**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Culture:** [[Culture — Aztecs]]

# Copper Age — Single Target

## Tlaloc — Rain Over the Fields

Rain, water, agricultural fertility, storms, lightning and mountains.

**City passive:** Irrigated agricultural production +5% · reservoirs store 10% more water · droughts cause fewer initial losses.
*Style: rain and agriculture.*

**Attack — Lightning of the Mountain.** Lightning damage · applies shock · reduces speed · **removes fire effects from the target** · additional damage if the enemy stands on wet terrain · metallic units suffer longer shock duration.

**Defense — Water of Life.** Restores health · removes burning · reduces fire damage · restores vigor · temporary regeneration · healing amplified near water or crops.

**Mobility — Current of the Rain.** Moves the target quickly · **crosses rivers, mud and flooded terrain** · removes slow · extinguishes burns · lightning resistance on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not transport siege engines · the destination must have a valid cell.

## Mixcoatl — Hunter of the Clouds

Hunting, stars, the Milky Way, storms and hunter-warriors.

**City passive:** Hunting and exploration +5% · explorers find animals and routes more easily · long-range troops gain a small bonus.
*Style: hunting, sky and exploration.*

**Attack — Arrow of the Cloud Serpent.** High damage · great range · ignores part of evasion · additional damage against animals, monsters and isolated units · **marks the target for tracking** · **at night damage and accuracy increase**.

**Defense — Stellar Cloak of the Hunter.** Increases evasion · reduces projectile damage · grants partial camouflage · prevents ambushes · **reveals nearby pursuers**.
*Night effect:* at night camouflage is amplified, duration increases and the target slowly recovers vigor.

**Mobility — Trail of the Stars.** Greatly increased speed · ignores forest, mountain and wild terrain penalties · reduces movement noise · **reveals traps along the path** · brief camouflage on arrival.

## Tezcatlipoca — Smoking Mirror

Night, destiny, power, conflict, sorcery, jaguars and the Smoking Mirror.

**City passive:** Prestige and espionage +5% · information about rivalries and conspiracies appears earlier · **some decisions may generate benefits accompanied by hidden consequences**.
*Style: power, destiny and intrigue.*

**Attack — Reflection of Ruin.** Spiritual damage · applies confusion · reduces accuracy · **copies part of the target's next damage and returns it against them** · removes camouflage and illusions · if the target is a leader, their command bonuses are reduced.

**Defense — Smoking Mirror.** Increases evasion · **the first attack received strikes an illusion** · reduces the chance of detection · grants resistance to mental effects · if the illusion is destroyed, the attacker suffers brief blindness.

**Mobility — Step of the Night Jaguar.** Moves the target quickly · passes through units and low obstacles · camouflage during the movement · **increased speed in forests and at night** · the first attack after arrival deals additional damage.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · does not transport siege engines · camouflage ends on attacking.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Quetzalcoatl — Breath of Civilization

Wind, knowledge, civilization, priesthood, fertility and the Feathered Serpent.

**City passive:** Research, trade and Prestige +10% · schools and markets produce additional Faith · diplomatic relations deteriorate more slowly.
*Style: knowledge, culture and trade.*

**Attack — Breath of the Feathered Serpent** (up to 3×3, line/cone/square). Wind damage · pushes enemies back · reduces accuracy · **removes smoke, fog and gases** · interrupts formations · flying units take additional damage · the epicenter may knock down light units.

**Defense — Wisdom of Tollan** (up to 3×3, square). Increases defense and magical resistance · removes confusion · grants resistance to fear · units keep formation · the first negative effect applied to each ally is nullified.
*Knowledge effect:* priests, scholars and commanders gain longer duration, **partial cooldown reduction** and an accuracy bonus.

**Mobility — Ascent of the Feathered Serpent** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies through the air · ignores terrain, units and low obstacles · crosses small walls · **they cannot be pushed back during the movement** · the landing disperses smoke and pushes light enemies · they arrive with speed and vision bonuses.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy machines are unaffected · anti-air barriers may prevent passage.

## Xochipilli — Flowers, Music and Games

Flowers, music, dance, games, art, pleasure and celebration.

**City passive:** Satisfaction and cultural production +10% · festivals cost less · artists, musicians and luxury artisans produce additional Prestige.
*Style: art, pleasure and festivals.*

**Attack — Dance of Ecstasy** (up to 3×3, square). Light spiritual damage · applies fascination · reduces accuracy · reduces attack speed · **enemies may interrupt their actions to dance** · commanders lose part of their leadership efficiency.
*Intensity:* **the lower the enemies' morale, the longer the fascination lasts and the greater the chance of interrupted actions**.

**Defense — Festival of Flowers** (up to 3×3, square). Restores morale · removes fear and sorrow · grants light regeneration · increases attack speed · reduces the duration of mental effects · **units at full morale gain additional evasion**.
*Cultural effect:* artists, musicians, priests and ceremonial units amplify the duration, grant additional Prestige or devotion and recover vigor faster.

**Mobility — Flowered Procession** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Increases speed · removes fear, slow and disorientation · passes through allied units · **maintains morale during a retreat** · they arrive with vigor restored · **the path travelled receives flowers granting allies a brief morale bonus**.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not transport heavy machines · **the effect ends if the formation takes heavy damage during the journey**.

## Xiuhtecuhtli — Fire of the Center

Fire, heat, time, the center of the world, homes and the continuity of the city.

**City passive:** Forges, kitchens and fuel production +10% · homes consume less fuel · the urban center gains Order and Faith bonuses.
*Style: household fire and continuity of the city.*

**Attack — Heart of the Burning Turquoise** (up to 3×3, square/cross/line). Fire damage · applies burning · damages buildings and siege engines · reduces armor · sets cells alight temporarily · the epicenter takes additional damage.
*Effect on time:* struck enemies have **cooldowns temporarily slowed**, suffer reduced attack speed and remain longer under burning.

**Defense — Central Fire** (up to 3×3, square or cross). Reduces cold and spiritual damage · prevents freezing · restores vigor · raises morale · **protects buildings against enemy fires** · enemies entering the area suffer burning.
*Central effect:* the central cell grants regeneration, amplifies defense and **acts as a rally point, preventing flight**.

**Mobility — Path of Embers** (origin up to 3×3, path of up to 3 cells). Increases unit speed · removes cold and slow · illuminates the route · **enemies crossing the trail suffer burning** · allies arrive resistant to fire · the trail remains active for a short period.
*Limitations:* does not cross great walls · **may set dry vegetation alight outside controlled areas** · units vulnerable to fire receive a shorter bonus duration.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Tonatiuh — Sun of Movement

The Sun, cosmic movement, war, energy and the maintenance of the current era.

**City passive:** Production, Faith and Military strength during the day +15% · great victories and solar ceremonies temporarily amplify the bonus · **maintaining the cult requires regular offerings**.
*Style: expansion, sun and continuous effort.*

**Attack — Fifth Sun** (up to 5×5, square/cross/circle within the limit). Solar and fire damage · very high damage at the epicenter · applies burning · blinds enemies · removes camouflage and illusions · reduces regeneration · shadowy and spiritual creatures take additional damage.
*Maintenance of the Sun:* **the more devotion accumulated before activation, the greater the damage, duration and reach of secondary effects**.
*Thematic cost:* the ability **consumes a large amount of accumulated devotion or offerings**.

**Defense — Shield of the Moving Sun** (up to 5×5, square). Reduces spiritual and darkness damage · grants regeneration · removes fear · raises morale · prevents freezing · **the first attack against each ally is partially reflected as solar damage** · buildings gain resistance against corruption and nocturnal effects.
*Ability cycle:* **Dawn** heals and removes negative effects · **Noon** grants the greatest damage reduction · **Dusk** grants speed and prepares the retreat.

**Mobility — March of the Solar Disk** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports or accelerates a large formation · ignores difficult terrain · removes fear, slow and darkness · reveals traps and hidden units · they arrive with attack and morale bonuses · **the path remains illuminated for reinforcements**.
*Daytime effect:* by day the range increases, speed is greater and the destination suffers a flash that blinds enemies.

## Mictlantecuhtli — Lord of Mictlán

Death, Mictlán, bones, night, the underworld and the destiny of the dead.

**City passive:** Mining and funerary administration +15% · deaths cause less instability · necropolises produce religious resources and reduce disease.
*Style: death, underground and continuity.*

**Attack — Hunger of Mictlán** (up to 5×5, square). Spiritual damage · reduces healing and morale · applies fear · **defeated units leave cursed bones** that reduce the speed and defense of nearby enemies · the epicenter may execute units with very low health.
*Underworld terrain:* the area stays cursed — **enemy resurrections are prevented**, allied spiritual units gain bonuses and enemies gradually lose vigor.

**Defense — Eternal Ossuary** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Reduces physical and spiritual damage · **fallen allies return as temporary skeletons** · deaths cause less morale loss · enemies cannot convert allied corpses · **each fallen unit temporarily strengthens the barrier** · living units gain resistance to fear.
*Limitation:* returned units cannot be healed · they vanish when the effect ends · **the maximum number depends on the bodies present within the area**.

**Mobility — Road of Mictlán** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the underworld · ignores terrain, walls and buildings · they cannot be intercepted · enemies near the destination suffer fear · they arrive camouflaged in shadow · spiritual units recover vigor.
*Cost:* living units lose a small amount of morale and receive brief slow on arrival — **they suffer neither if accompanied by a priest**.

## Huitzilopochtli — March of the Warrior Sun

War, the Sun, conquest, military power, Mexica destiny and the protection of Tenochtitlán.

**City passive:** Offensive Military strength and recruitment speed +15% · conquests grant Faith and Satisfaction · **long periods without conflict gradually reduce religious fervor**.
*Style: war, conquest and mobilization.*

**Attack — War of the Sun** (up to 5×5, square). Greatly increases damage · increases attack speed · grants resistance to fear · attacks apply solar damage · **defeated enemies restore allies' vigor** · commanders amplify the bonuses · units at the center receive the greatest offensive strength.
*Escalation of conquest:* each enemy defeated during the ult temporarily increases speed, restores morale and **slightly extends the duration, up to a limit**.
*Risk:* defense is reduced · units may pursue enemies beyond their original position · **voluntary retreat ends the unit's offensive bonus**.

**Defense — Hummingbird of the South** (up to 5×5, square). High resistance · reduces projectile damage · prevents fear · fatally struck units keep fighting for a few moments · **fallen warriors generate spiritual hummingbirds that restore health and morale to nearby allies** · the first attack against each commander deals reduced damage.
*Limitation:* each unit can avoid death only once · **part of the avoided damage returns at the end if the unit is not healed**.

**Mobility — March to Tenochtitlán** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports or accelerates troops toward a strategic objective · ignores difficult terrain, shallow lakes and flooded areas · keeps the formation · prevents fear and disorganization during the journey · they arrive with attack, morale and speed bonuses · **if the destination is an allied city, temple or fortification, they gain additional protection**.
*Offensive condition:* if the destination is near an enemy position, the landing pushes enemies back, the first charge deals additional damage and units cannot be slowed for a few moments.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| God | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Tlaloc** — Dry Sky | Irrigated agriculture −10% · reservoirs store 20% less water · **droughts cause amplified losses from the very first moment** |
| **Mixcoatl** — Hidden Stars | Hunting and exploration −10% · explorers find neither animals nor routes · long-range troops lose the bonus |
| **Tezcatlipoca** — Broken Mirror | Prestige and espionage −10% · conspiracies surface only too late · **decisions produce the hidden consequences without delivering the benefits** |
| **Quetzalcoatl** — Breath Withdrawn | Research, trade and Prestige −20% · schools and markets generate no Faith · **diplomatic relations deteriorate twice as fast** |
| **Xochipilli** — Withered Flowers | Satisfaction and cultural production −20% · festivals cost double · artists, musicians and luxury artisans generate no Prestige |
| **Xiuhtecuhtli** — Fire Extinguished | Forges, kitchens and fuel production −20% · homes consume more fuel · **the urban center loses Order and Faith** |
| **Tonatiuh** — Sun Halted | Daytime production, Faith and Military strength −30% · victories and solar ceremonies amplify nothing · **the demand for regular offerings remains** |
| **Mictlantecuhtli** — Mictlán Opened | Mining and funerary administration −30% · deaths cause amplified instability · **necropolises stop generating religious resources and begin spreading disease** |
| **Huitzilopochtli** — March Interrupted | Offensive Military strength and recruitment speed −30% · conquests grant neither Faith nor Satisfaction · **religious fervor plummets even during an active campaign** |

## Strategic Identity

**Tlaloc** shock, healing, water and movement in flooded terrain · **Mixcoatl** precision, hunting, night vision and mobility in wild terrain · **Tezcatlipoca** confusion, damage reflection, illusions and infiltration · **Quetzalcoatl** wind control, protection through knowledge and aerial mobility · **Xochipilli** morale, fascination, festivals and collective movement · **Xiuhtecuhtli** fire, defense of the urban center and creation of hazardous routes · **Tonatiuh** solar damage, cyclical protection and advance of large formations · **Mictlantecuhtli** death, temporary units, fear and crossing through the underworld · **Huitzilopochtli** aggressive war, military resilience and conquest-oriented mobility.
