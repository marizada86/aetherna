**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Culture:** [[Culture — Greeks]]

# Copper Age — Single Target

## Demeter — Gift of the Harvests

Agriculture, fertility of the land, harvest, seasons and sustenance of the population.

**City passive:** Agricultural production +5% · granaries preserve food longer · losses of stored food −10%.
*Style: agriculture and food security.*

**Attack — Shackles of the Harvest.** A plant grows around a single enemy. Light initial damage · immobilizes the target · reduces its defense · gradually drains its vigor · mounted units temporarily lose mobility bonuses.

**Defense — Fruit of Demeter.** Divine food to an ally. Restores health and vigor · removes hunger and exhaustion · grants temporary regeneration · healing amplified if the target is defending a farm or granary.

**Mobility — Passage Between the Fields.** Moves the target to a valid cell within range · ignores penalties from fields, crops and low vegetation · removes slow · increased speed after arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls, does not cross large bodies of water, does not transport siege engines.

## Artemis — Guardian of the Wild Lands

Hunting, the bow, wild nature, the Moon, animals and protection of the young.

**City passive:** Hunting production +5% · forests and wild areas recover resources 10% faster · explorers move faster outside the city.
*Style: hunting, exploration and conservation.*

**Attack — Arrow of the Huntress.** High damage · great range · ignores part of evasion · additional damage against animals, monsters and isolated units · reveals the target for a period.

**Defense — Protection of the Moon.** Increases evasion · reduces projectile damage · grants partial camouflage · increases resistance against creatures and animals · at night the target slowly recovers health.

**Mobility — Step of the Doe.** Greatly increased speed · ignores forest, thicket and wild terrain penalties · crosses small natural obstacles · reduces movement noise · brief camouflage after stopping.

## Apollo — Light of Harmony

The Sun, music, healing, prophecy, the bow, knowledge and harmony.

**City passive:** Faith and Health +5% · houses of healing work 10% faster · musical festivals produce additional Prestige.
*Style: health, art and religion.*

**Attack — Solar Arrow.** High damage · applies burning · temporarily blinds · removes camouflage and invisibility · additional damage against shadowy or spiritual creatures.

**Defense — Restoring Hymn.** Restores health · removes poison, disease and bleeding · temporarily increases accuracy · grants resistance against fear · healing amplified if the target is near death.

**Mobility — Chariot of Dawn.** Moves the target quickly · removes slow and darkness · reveals the path travelled · the target cannot be caught by surprise while moving · temporary vision bonus on arrival.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Ares — Fury of War

Violence, fury, courage, brutality and the chaos of combat.

**City passive:** Offensive military strength +10% · military victories grant Faith and Prestige · defeats cause 10% more Satisfaction loss.
*Style: aggressive conquest.*

**Attack — Frenzy of War** (3×3, square). Greatly increases damage · increases attack speed · increases movement speed toward enemies · grants resistance to fear · **partially reduces the defense of affected units** · units that eliminate enemies recover vigor.

**Defense — Last Blood** (3×3, square). Allies cannot die immediately · fatally wounded units remain at minimum health · part of the damage dealt restores health · resistance against knockback · **on ending, each unit takes part of the fatal damage avoided**.
*Limitation:* each unit can prevent only one death per activation.

**Mobility — Charge of Ares** (origin 3×3, path in a line of up to 3 cells). Moves units quickly forward · knocks down enemies in the path · breaks formations · destroys light barricades · damage bonus on the first attack after arrival.
*Restriction:* direction cannot be changed after activation.

## Athena — Strategy of the Polis

Strategy, wisdom, defensive war, craftsmanship, justice and protection of cities.

**City passive:** Research speed and defensive construction +10% · units defending walls or strategic points +5% efficiency · missions allowing a diplomatic or strategic solution grant better rewards.
*Style: planning, defense and knowledge.*

**Attack — Calculated Flaw** (3×3, square). Reduces enemy armor · removes formation bonuses · reduces evasion · reveals hidden units · allied attacks against the targets deal additional damage · the central enemy takes the greatest penalty.

**Defense — Aegis of Athena** (3×3, square or line). Reduces physical damage · blocks part of projectiles · increases magical resistance · prevents formation disorder · enemies entering the area suffer momentary fear.

**Mobility — Perfect Repositioning** (origin and destination 3×3). Moves units keeping relative positions · allows swapping the front line with the rear · reduces damage taken during repositioning · defensive bonus after arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross great walls, does not transport structures, heavy machines take more space at the destination.

## Hephaestus — Divine Forges

Fire, the forge, metallurgy, craftsmanship, volcanoes and the creation of automatons.

**City passive:** Production of tools, weapons and armor +10% · equipment consumes 5% less metal · smiths gain experience faster.
*Style: industry and metallurgy.*

**Attack — Rupture of the Forge** (3×3, line/cross/square). Fire damage · applies burning · reduces armor · damages siege engines and buildings · the struck cells become hazardous terrain for a period · the epicenter deals additional damage.

**Defense — Living Armor** (3×3, square). Increases unit armor · slowly repairs weapons and equipment · restores siege engine integrity · reduces fire damage · metallic units become resistant to knockback.

**Mobility — March of the Automatons** (origin and destination 3×3). Increases the speed of heavy units · transports light siege engines · ignores part of load penalties · removes barricades and small obstacles from the path · units arrive with equipment partially repaired.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Zeus — Authority of the Heavens

Sky, thunder, authority, justice, sovereignty and rule over the gods.

**City passive:** Order and Prestige +15% · rulers and administrative buildings have 10% greater effects · clans with high loyalty grant the city an additional bonus.
*Style: central government and authority.*

**Attack — Wrath of Olympus** (5×5, square). Lightning strikes multiple enemies · units at the epicenter take very high damage · metallic units take additional damage · commanders and siege engines are more likely to be struck · struck enemies suffer shock · enemy morale drops · the storm lingers for a few moments.

**Defense — Decree of the King of Gods** (5×5, square). Increases ally defense · grants resistance to fear and control · amplifies commander bonuses · reduces enemy leadership bonuses · the first enemy to attack within the area is struck by lightning · administrative and defensive buildings gain additional resistance.

**Mobility — Ascent of the Eagle** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports units through the air · ignores walls, rivers and elevation · units cannot be attacked during part of the journey · the landing causes fear · nearby enemies are pushed back · allies arrive with vision and speed bonuses.

## Poseidon — Dominion of the Waters

Seas, earthquakes, horses, sea storms and the destructive power of water.

**City passive:** Fishing, port and maritime trade production +15% · coastal buildings complete 20% faster · floods cause less damage, though severe storms may still occur.
*Style: coastal cities and naval trade.*

**Attack — Destroying Wave** (5×5, line/cone/square). High damage · pushes enemies back · knocks down light units · damages buildings and siege engines · **extinguishes fires** · temporarily turns the terrain into a flooded area · the wave's epicenter causes the greatest impact.

**Defense — Fortress of Tides** (5×5, square or ring). Reduces physical and fire damage · prevents enemy charges · pushes back enemies attempting to enter · extinguishes burns · slowly restores coastal or naval buildings · aquatic units gain additional regeneration.

**Mobility — Currents of the Trident** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports troops and vessels · works on seas, rivers, canals and temporarily flooded terrain · greatly increases speed · ignores aquatic blockades · lets naval units cross small flooded land areas · allies arrive resistant to knockback.

## Hades — Riches of the Underground

The underworld, the dead, subterranean riches, oaths and the inevitability of death.

**City passive:** Mine and quarry production +15% · deaths and funerals cause 20% less Order loss · necropolises produce a small amount of Faith and Prestige.
*Style: mining, wealth and administration of death.*

**Attack — Gates of the Underworld** (5×5, square or circle within the limit). Spiritual damage · reduces morale · applies fear · **defeated units generate temporary shades that attack the dead's former allies** · the epicenter applies a curse reducing healing · the area stays contaminated for a few moments.

**Defense — Dominion of the Dead** (5×5, square). Fallen allies return as temporary shades retaining part of their attributes · living units gain resistance to fear · deaths cause less morale loss · shades cannot be healed and vanish when the effect ends.

**Mobility — Road of the Underworld** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports units through the underworld · ignores walls, terrain and buildings · enemies cannot intercept the route · the exit causes fear · nearby enemies temporarily lose vision · allies arrive camouflaged in shadow.
*Thematic cost:* transported units lose a small amount of health or morale; spiritual units are exempt.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| God | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Demeter** — Hunger of Demeter | Agricultural production −10% · granaries spoil food faster · losses of stored food +20% |
| **Artemis** — Barren Grove | Hunting production −10% · **forests and wild areas do not recover resources** · explorers move slower outside the city |
| **Apollo** — Plague of Apollo | Faith and Health −10% · houses of healing work 20% slower · musical festivals generate no Prestige |
| **Ares** — Cowardice | Offensive military strength −20% · military victories grant neither Faith nor Prestige · defeats cause double Satisfaction loss |
| **Athena** — Unguarded Polis | Research speed and defensive construction −20% · units on walls and strategic points lose 10% efficiency · diplomatic missions give worse rewards |
| **Hephaestus** — Cold Forges | Production of tools, weapons and armor −20% · equipment consumes 10% more metal · **smiths stop gaining experience** |
| **Zeus** — Silent Sky | Order and Prestige −30% · rulers and administrative buildings have reduced effects · **loyal clans stop granting bonuses and may contest the government** |
| **Poseidon** — Contrary Tide | Fishing, ports and maritime trade −30% · coastal buildings 40% slower · floods cause amplified damage |
| **Hades** — Sealed Underground | Mines and quarries −30% · deaths and funerals cause double Order loss · necropolises stop generating Faith and Prestige |

## Strategic Identity

**Demeter** sustain, healing and individual control through vegetation · **Artemis** precision, stealth and mastery of wild terrain · **Apollo** healing, revelation, light and combat against spiritual threats · **Ares** aggressive attacks, charges and short-term survival · **Athena** organized defense, enemy analysis and repositioning · **Hephaestus** metallurgy, siege engines, repairs and fire control · **Zeus** wide destruction, authority and aerial movement · **Poseidon** terrain control, coastal combat and aquatic movement · **Hades** fear, temporary dead and infiltration through the underworld.
