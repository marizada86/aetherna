# Descent — Westerners

**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Cultures:** [[Culture — Norse]] · [[Culture — Greeks]]

**Stone Age** package for every city of Western Descent. Identical for whoever later becomes Norse or Greek — the two cultures only diverge in the Copper Age, with the choice of the first deity.

See [[Culture System]] for the layer mechanic and [[Era Progression]] for generic advancement rules.

## Stone Age — Western Settlement

**Maximum population:** 20 citizens.

### Identity

A tribal community of extended families and small informal clans, settled on rugged, coastal or cold-forest terrain. The population depends on hunting, fishing, gathering, rudimentary herding of goats, sheep and pigs, early grain cultivation, timber extraction and direct barter.

There is no polis, hall, þing, agora, formal citizenship or writing.

### Founding

Five houses built close together in a habitable zone unlock the **Gathering Point**. On raising it, players choose the Descent and the city becomes Western.

### Social Organization

Families · informal clans · elders · hunters · herders · fishers · rudimentary farmers · artisans · communal warriors.

Clans influence marriages, food distribution, family occupations, support for the chief, funerary rites, hunting expeditions, settlement defense and disputes over land and animals.

### Government

Tribal chieftaincy decided at the Gathering Point by **in-person voting**.

**Institutions:** community chief · informal council of elders · circle of families · spokesperson for each clan.

Decisions rest on tradition, reputation, leadership capacity, family support and survival needs. The assembly has no legal power — it functions as a meeting to solve collective problems.

### Religion

**None.** The Stone Age has no religion in any Descent: no temple, no priesthood, no patron deity, no Faith generation, no religious buildings.

Burials exist as a **social custom**, with no mechanical effect. The city's spiritual life begins only in the Copper Age, with the first temple.

### Economy

Direct barter.

**Resources:** grain · fish · meat · hides · timber · stone · clay · wool · leather · bone · herbs · honey.

**Production:** stone tools · bone weapons · fur clothing · rope · baskets · rudimentary pottery · simple textiles · small boats · smoked meat and fish.

### Buildings

**Housing:** family hut · communal house · hide shelter · herders' shelter · fishers' hut · temporary camp.

**Food:** gathering field · hunting camp · fishing spot · primitive garden · simple pen · primitive granary · rudimentary smokehouse.

**Production:** stone workshop · bone workshop · wood workshop · rudimentary tannery · weaving area · rudimentary pottery kiln · communal fire · rudimentary shipyard.

**Administration:** **Gathering Point** · chief's house · circle of elders · boundary marker.

**Defense:** palisade · watchpost · simple gate · rudimentary tower · warriors' shelter · shallow trench.

### Military Forces

Temporarily levied citizens. There is no standing army.

Hunter · slinger · tribal spearman · warrior with club or stone axe · scout · defender with wooden shield.

### Cultural trait — Blood Ties

Citizens of the same clan work and fight better when they remain close together.

**Benefits:** morale bonus · greater efficiency in shared tasks · faster stamina recovery · greater resistance to fear · faster recovery from internal disputes.

**Risks:** the loss of an important member affects the morale of the whole clan · rival clans nearby generate conflict · offenses can start cycles of vengeance.

### Stone Age Missions

1. **Found the settlement** — build the first houses, establish a water source, create a fishing or cultivation spot.
2. **Raise the Gathering Point** — gather five houses close together, build the site and **choose the Descent**.
3. **Feed the families** — produce enough food, build a granary, avoid famine for a full cycle.
4. **Prepare for winter** — store food and firewood, produce hides and clothing, smoke meat and fish.
5. **Gather the clans** — secure the support of the main clans, resolve a rivalry, organize a common celebration.
6. **Protect the settlement** — build a palisade, create watchposts, organize defenders.
7. **Build the first boat** — gather timber, create a canoe or simple boat, explore the coast or a river.
8. **Discover copper** — locate a deposit, gather ore, produce the first metal object.

### Unavailable Systems

Temple · patron deity · priesthood · Faith · Great Hall · agora · þing · citizenship · clan seat · clan missions · currency · formal government · standing army · organized trade · philosophy · sagas · jarls.

### Advancing to the Copper Age

- Sustain a stable population.
- Secure food and water.
- Store resources for winter.
- Keep clans relatively stable.
- Hold recognized leadership.
- Build a workshop.
- Build a functional boat.
- Discover copper and produce a metal tool.
- **Gathering Point built and Descent chosen.**

## What Happens in the Copper Age

On advancing, the city builds its first temple and chooses **one of six deities** — the three of Norse Copper or the three of Greek Copper. That choice defines the culture and activates the entire cultural package.

| Choice | Resulting culture | Copper deities |
|---|---|---|
| Freyr, Uller or Bragi | Norse | [[Pantheon — Norse]] |
| Demeter, Artemis or Apollo | Greeks | [[Pantheon — Greeks]] |

Systems that activate with Norse culture: Great Hall · Honor · Gifts · jarls.
Systems that activate with Greek culture: Agora · archon · limited assembly.
