# Descent — Africans

**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Cultures:** [[Culture — Egyptians]] · [[Culture — Yoruba]]

**Stone Age** package for every city of African Descent. Identical for whoever later becomes Egyptian or Yoruba.

See [[Culture System]] and [[Era Progression]].

## Stone Age — African Settlement

**Maximum population:** 20 citizens.

### Identity

A community of extended families and lineages settled between river and forest. The population depends on fishing, hunting, gathering, rudimentary cultivation, early animal husbandry, timber extraction, medicinal herbs and direct barter.

Families share labor, food, protection, traditions and **responsibility for vulnerable members**.

There is no temple, writing, organized market or formal government.

### Founding

Five houses built close together in a habitable zone unlock the **Gathering Point**. On raising it, players choose the Descent and the city becomes African.

### Social Organization

Extended families · lineages · informal clans · elders · rudimentary farmers · hunters · fishers · gatherers · animal breeders · artisans · traditional healers · communal warriors.

Clans influence marriages, professions, food division, funerary rites, occupation of cultivation areas, use of the riverbanks, support for the leader, relations with other families and dispute resolution.

### Government

Community lineage leadership, decided at the Gathering Point by **in-person voting**.

**Institutions:** community leader · informal council of elders · family spokespeople · community circle · **guardian of traditions** · observer of the waters.

Decisions are made by **negotiation**, elders' experience, personal prestige, lineage support and survival needs.

### Religion

**None.** No temple, priesthood, patron deity, Faith or religious buildings. Burials are a social custom with no mechanical effect.

### Economy

Direct barter.

**Resources:** tubers · grain · fruit · fish · meat · reeds · timber · stone · clay · fiber · leather · herbs · honey · nuts and seeds · water.

**Production:** stone tools · baskets · rope · fishing nets · mats · rudimentary pottery · simple textiles · wood and stone weapons · reed boats · dried food · **natural dyes** · herbal remedies.

### Buildings

**Housing:** reed hut · mud hut · family house · communal house · fishers' shelter · hunters' shelter · **family courtyard** · temporary camp.

**Food:** rudimentary field · primitive garden · gathering field · fishing spot · hunting camp · simple pen · primitive granary · food drying area.

**Production:** stone workshop · bone workshop · rudimentary pottery · weaving area · basket workshop · simple tannery · **herb preparation area** · reed boat yard · communal fire.

**Infrastructure:** shallow well · earth ditch · dirt path · rudimentary wooden bridge · communal warehouse · water distribution point · waste area.

**Administration:** **Gathering Point** · leader's house · circle of elders · **community courtyard** · territorial marker.

**Defense:** palisade · barrier of reeds, wood and thorns · watchpost · rudimentary tower · simple gate · warriors' shelter.

### Military Forces

Temporarily levied citizens. There is no standing army.

Hunter · armed fisher · slinger · tribal spearman · rudimentary archer · warrior with club · scout · defender with wooden shield.

### Cultural trait — Lineage Courtyard

Houses belonging to the same lineage can be arranged around a **common courtyard**.

**Benefits:** greater cohesion · more efficient household production · better care of children and elders · faster morale recovery · greater food distribution.

**Risks:** **epidemics strike several neighboring families at once** · family rivalries affect the whole cluster · overcrowding reduces hygiene and satisfaction.

### Stone Age Missions

1. **Found the community** — build dwellings, establish a water source, create a cultivation, fishing or hunting spot.
2. **Raise the Gathering Point** — gather five houses close together, build the site and **choose the Descent**.
3. **Organize the common courtyard** — build houses close together, create a meeting space, distribute tasks among families.
4. **Feed the families** — produce food, build a granary, avoid famine for a full cycle.
5. **Gather the lineages** — secure the support of the main groups, resolve a rivalry, organize a community celebration.
6. **Learn from the forest** — identify useful plants, find medicinal herbs, establish a sustainable gathering area.
7. **Protect the community** — build a palisade, create watchposts, organize defenders.
8. **Discover copper** — find ore, gather material, produce the first metal object.

### Unavailable Systems

Temple · patron deity · priesthood · Faith · writing · scribes · organized market · clan seat · clan missions · formal council · royal government · taxation · standing army · professional guilds · urban districts · monuments.

### Advancing to the Copper Age

- Sustain a stable population.
- Secure food and water.
- Build a granary.
- Hold recognized leadership.
- Keep the lineages relatively stable.
- Build a workshop.
- Discover copper and produce a metal tool.
- Create a permanent barter area.
- **Gathering Point built and Descent chosen.**
