# Copper Age — Single Target

## Oxóssi — Lord of the Forest and Plenty

Hunting, the forests, plenty, precision and the knowledge needed to find resources.

**City passive:** Hunting, gathering and forest exploitation +5% · explorers find resources more easily · **sustainable exploitation reduces forest depletion**.
*Style: forest, hunting and discovery.*

**Attack — Arrow of the Forest.** High damage · great range · ignores part of evasion · additional damage against animals, monsters and isolated units · reveals the target for a period · **if the target is inside a forest, the arrow ignores part of the cover**.
*Hunting effect:* if the target has low health, damage is amplified and its defeat grants temporary vision around its position.

**Defense — Instinct of the Hunter.** Increases evasion · reveals nearby traps · **prevents the target from being caught by surprise** · increases resistance against ranged attacks · the first enemy attack deals reduced damage.
*In wild territory:* inside forests or dense vegetation, the target gains partial camouflage and the duration increases.

**Mobility — Step of the Hunter.** Greatly increased speed · ignores forest and vegetation penalties · crosses small natural obstacles · reduces movement noise · camouflage on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not transport siege engines · **the bonus is smaller in open urban areas**.

## Oxum — Wealth of the Fresh Waters

Fresh waters, fertility, prosperity, beauty, affection and abundance.

**City passive:** Population growth and fresh water production +5% · markets near rivers produce additional Prestige · festivals temporarily raise Satisfaction.
*Style: fertility, prosperity and rivers.*

**Attack — Mirror of the Waters.** Moderate spiritual damage · reduces accuracy · applies fascination · the target takes longer to begin attacks · **part of the target's next damage is reflected back at it** · removes effects of fury or excessive aggression.

**Defense — Bath of Oxum.** Restores health · removes burning, poison and ritual impurity · **restores morale** · temporary regeneration · reduces the duration of fear and sorrow effects.
*Near fresh water:* close to a river, spring or lake, healing is amplified and regeneration lasts longer.

**Mobility — Golden Current.** Moves the target to a valid cell · crosses rivers and narrow canals · removes slow · restores part of vigor · briefly greater evasion on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not cross large stretches of ocean · does not transport structures or heavy machines.

## Obaluaê / Omolu — Lord of Healing and Disease

The earth, diseases, epidemics, healing and the transformation brought by suffering.

**City passive:** Disease resistance +5% · houses of healing consume 10% fewer herbs · **overcoming an epidemic grants Faith and Prestige**.
*Style: health and endurance.*

**Attack — Straw of Pestilence.** Gradual damage · reduces healing received · reduces speed · lowers physical resistance · the target temporarily loses regeneration bonuses.
*Worsening:* the lower the target's health, the greater the disease damage and the lower its healers' efficiency.
*Restriction:* **the disease remains only on the original target and does not spread**, respecting the absence of AoE in the Copper Age.

**Defense — Healing by the Straws.** Removes disease and poison · restores health gradually · **temporary immunity to epidemics** · reduces spiritual damage and corruption effects.
*Transformation of suffering:* **the more negative effects removed, the greater the final healing and the temporary resistance granted**.

**Mobility — Passage Beneath the Earth.** Repositions the target to a valid cell · passes through units and low obstacles · removes entrapment and immobilization · **the target is hidden during the movement** · resistance to disease and physical damage on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross deep walls · does not transport siege engines · cannot end in deep water.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Ogum — Path of Iron

Iron, metallurgy, war, labor, tools and the opening of paths.

**City passive:** Tool and weapon production +10% · roads are built 10% faster · troops equipped with metal gain a Strength bonus.
*Style: metallurgy, labor and war.*

**Attack — Cut of the Seven Paths** (up to 3×3, line/cross/square). High physical damage · ignores part of armor · damages buildings and siege engines · destroys light barricades · applies bleeding · the epicenter takes additional damage.
*Against equipment:* units with metal armor or weapons temporarily lose part of their defense and **suffer reduced equipment durability**.

**Defense — Cuirass of Ogum** (up to 3×3, square). Increases armor · **partially repairs weapons and shields** · grants resistance to projectiles · prevents knockback · reduces damage taken by siege engines · units with metal equipment gain additional protection.
*Counterattack:* the first enemy to attack a protected unit in melee suffers return damage.

**Mobility — Opening of the Paths** (origin up to 3×3, path in a line of up to 3 cells). Moves units quickly forward · destroys barricades and vegetation in the way · **removes physical traps** · passes through light enemy formations · keeps the formation organized · attack bonus on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · does not turn · very resistant obstacles halt the advance.

## Iansã / Oyá — Lady of Winds and Storms

Winds, storms, lightning, transformation, movement and the spirits of the dead.

**City passive:** Movement speed of workers and troops +10% · fires and storm effects are resolved faster · **political changes and leader replacements cause less Order loss**.
*Style: mobility, storms and transformation.*

**Attack — Gale of the Eguns** (up to 3×3, square or cone). Spiritual and lightning damage · pushes enemies back · reduces accuracy · disorganizes formations · applies fear · **spirits strike enemies with low health**.
*Transformation:* enemies defeated during the ability generate an additional gust and briefly increase the speed of affected allied units.

**Defense — Wall of the Nine Winds** (up to 3×3, square or ring). Deflects projectiles · reduces ranged damage · **prevents the spread of smoke and poisons** · increases evasion · pushes back enemies attempting to enter · extinguishes small fires or drives them from the area.
*Against spirits:* allies gain resistance to fear and hostile spirits have their speed reduced.

**Mobility — Ride of the Storm** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports units quickly · ignores difficult terrain and units · crosses small obstacles · they cannot be slowed during the journey · the landing pushes enemies back · they arrive with speed and evasion bonuses.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy siege engines are unaffected · great magical barriers block passage.

## Exu — Lord of Paths and Exchanges

Paths, crossroads, communication, movement, negotiation, ambiguity and the consequences of choices.

**City passive:** Trade and route range +10% · merchants may find rare offers · **commercial decisions have more variable results, positive or negative**.
*Style: communication, opportunity and unpredictability.*

**Attack — Crossroads of Choices** (up to 3×3, cross or square). Applies confusion · **enemies may pick incorrect targets** · movement orders may be diverted to adjacent cells · reduces communication with commanders · temporarily disables formation bonuses · units at the center take an additional accuracy penalty.
*Forced choice:* each enemy struck receives one consequence — loss of speed, damage reduction, involuntary repositioning or ability interruption.

**Defense — Guardian of the Passage** (up to 3×3, cross). **Allies may cross the area without being blocked** · enemies suffer reduced speed · the first attack against each ally is deflected · traps and hidden units are revealed · **silence and ability-blocking effects are removed** · commanders' messages and bonuses cannot be interrupted.
*Counterattack:* enemies attempting to trap or block allies are marked and take additional damage from the next allied source.

**Mobility — Impossible Crossroads** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports units between the two areas · ignores obstacles and small walls · keeps relative positions where possible · removes entrapment · they arrive with a speed bonus · **the portal can be used in both directions for a short period**.
*Controlled risk:* on crossing, each unit may receive a random **beneficial** effect — speed, attack, brief camouflage or vigor. **There are no random effects that eliminate or incapacitate allies.**

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Xangô — Justice of Thunder

Thunder, lightning, fire, royal power, justice and punishment of transgressors.

**City passive:** Order and defensive Military strength +15% · tribunals produce Prestige · **clans that violate laws or agreements lose influence faster**.
*Style: justice, authority and power.*

**Attack — Axe of Thunder** (up to 5×5, square/cross/line). High lightning damage · the epicenter takes additional physical damage · stuns directly struck units · metallic units take amplified damage · siege engines may be temporarily disabled · lowers morale · **additional bolts strike enemies marked as criminals or traitors**.
*Judgment:* enemies who have broken treaties, attacked civilians, betrayed allies or disobeyed legitimate orders take an additional defense and resistance penalty.

**Defense — Tribunal of Xangô** (up to 5×5, square). Strongly increases defense · grants resistance to fear and confusion · **prevents allies from being executed** · reduces damage dealt by enemies who started the confrontation · the first aggressor against each ally is struck by lightning · commanders grant greater bonuses within the area.
*Balanced justice:* **allies who attack civilians, the surrendered or neutral targets immediately lose the protection and may be struck by Xangô's own judgment.**

**Mobility — Path of Thunder** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports units instantly · ignores terrain, rivers and obstacles · **the destination is struck by thunder** · nearby enemies are stunned or pushed back · they arrive with attack, morale and resistance bonuses · allied metallic units gain additional attack speed.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · very heavy machines need extra space at the destination · insulating or magical barriers may block it.

## Oxalá — Foundation of Creation

Creation, the shaping of humanity, peace, purity, balance, ancestral authority and protection.

**City passive:** Satisfaction, Faith and construction speed +15% · new districts begin with additional stability · prolonged periods of peace reduce the cost of urban expansion.
*Style: creation, peace and city formation.*

**Attack — Decree of Creation** (up to 5×5, square). Moderate spiritual damage · **strongly reduces damage dealt by enemies** · removes fury effects · temporarily disarms common units · reduces attack speed · **prevents new summons within the area** · corrupted or artificial creatures take additional damage.
*Pacification:* enemies with low morale break off combat, cannot attack for a short period and may retreat voluntarily.

**Defense — Alá of Purity** (up to 5×5, square or circle within the limit). Strongly reduces all damage taken · removes poison, disease, fear and corruption · gradual regeneration · prevents mind control · buildings cannot be destroyed during the first moments · units with low health gain additional protection.
*Harmony:* **while allies do not initiate attacks**, regeneration is amplified, damage reduction increases and the area recovers stability faster.

**Mobility — First Path** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Creates a crossable path between origin and destination · temporarily removes natural obstacles · crosses rivers, moats and ruined terrain · allies move without losing formation · removes fear, disorientation and slow · **the path remains available to reinforcements for the duration**.
*Restriction:* does not cross areas protected by equivalent powers · cannot be used to destroy buildings directly · **the terrain returns to its previous state when the effect ends**.

## Iemanjá — Mother of the Waters

Salt waters, motherhood, protection, care, fertility and the strength of the seas.

**City passive:** Maritime production and population growth +15% · ports increase the Satisfaction of coastal districts · sea voyages suffer fewer losses.
*Style: sea, protection and population.*

**Attack — Tide of the Great Mother** (up to 5×5, line/cone/square). High damage · pushes enemies back · knocks down light units · extinguishes fires · damages siege engines · **removes positive effects based on fire** · temporarily turns the terrain into a flooded area.
*Force of the tide:* units near the epicenter are dragged to another cell, lose part of their vigor and suffer a temporary speed reduction.

**Defense — Embrace of Iemanjá** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Gradual healing · reduces physical and fire damage · removes burns and bleeding · raises morale · allies with low health gain an additional shield · **projectiles lose force crossing the area** · aquatic units gain amplified regeneration.
*Maternal protection:* the first unit that would take fatal damage remains at minimum health, **is moved to a safe cell within the area** and receives immediate healing. The effect may occur once per unit per activation, subject to final balancing.

**Mobility — Paths of the Great Sea** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports troops and vessels · ignores rivers, coasts and flooded areas · temporarily crosses parts of the terrain on a current of water · they cannot be intercepted during part of the route · they arrive with vigor restored · removes burns and slow · the destination is temporarily flooded.
*Coastal effect:* when origin and destination are connected to the sea, the range increases, **vessels may also be transported** and units receive a shield on arrival.

## Wrath of the Abandoned Orisha

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| Orisha | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Oxóssi** — Closed Forest | Hunting, gathering and forest exploitation −10% · explorers find no resources · **forests deplete even under sustainable exploitation** |
| **Oxum** — Murky Waters | Population growth and fresh water production −10% · markets by rivers generate no Prestige · festivals do not raise Satisfaction |
| **Obaluaê/Omolu** — Plague of Omolu | Disease resistance −10% · houses of healing consume 20% more herbs · **overcoming an epidemic grants neither Faith nor Prestige** |
| **Ogum** — Broken Iron | Tool and weapon production −20% · roads 20% slower · troops equipped with metal lose Strength |
| **Iansã/Oyá** — Stilled Winds | Worker and troop speed −20% · fires and storms take twice as long to resolve · **leadership changes cause amplified Order loss** |
| **Exu** — Closed Paths | Trade and route range −20% · merchants find no rare offers · **commercial decisions produce only negative results** |
| **Xangô** — Empty Throne | Order and defensive Military strength −30% · tribunals generate no Prestige · **clans that violate laws or agreements lose no influence at all** |
| **Oxalá** — Creation Interrupted | Satisfaction, Faith and construction speed −30% · **new districts are born unstable** · periods of peace raise the cost of urban expansion |
| **Iemanjá** — Raging Sea | Maritime production and population growth −30% · **ports reduce the Satisfaction of coastal districts** · sea voyages suffer amplified losses |

## Strategic Identity

**Oxóssi** precision, exploration, stealth and forest combat · **Oxum** healing, emotional recovery, enchantment and movement by fresh waters · **Obaluaê/Omolu** disease, purification, regeneration and passage through the earth · **Ogum** physical damage, metallurgy, siege engines and frontal advance · **Iansã/Oyá** control by wind, storms, spirits and high mobility · **Exu** confusion, communication, portals and positioning manipulation · **Xangô** lightning damage, justice, counterattack and decisive entries · **Oxalá** extreme protection, purification, pacification and path creation · **Iemanjá** collective healing, aquatic control and transport of large formations.
