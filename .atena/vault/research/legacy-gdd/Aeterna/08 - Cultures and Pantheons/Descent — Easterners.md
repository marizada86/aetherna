# Descent — Easterners

**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Cultures:** [[Culture — Mesopotamia]] · [[Culture — Sān Jiào]]

**Stone Age** package for every city of Eastern Descent. Identical for whoever later becomes Mesopotamian or Sān Jiào.

See [[Culture System]] and [[Era Progression]].

## Stone Age — Eastern Settlement

**Maximum population:** 20 citizens.

### Identity

A small community settled near a river or fertile floodplain. The population depends on rudimentary cultivation, fishing, animal husbandry, gathering, hunting, clay, reeds, bamboo and direct barter.

The inhabitants begin digging **small ditches** to bring water to the fields — the embryo of the irrigation that will define both Eastern cultures.

There is no temple, writing, organized market, bureaucracy or formal government.

### Founding

Five houses built close together in a habitable zone unlock the **Gathering Point**. On raising it, players choose the Descent and the city becomes Eastern.

### Social Organization

Extended families · informal clans · elders · farmers · fishers · herders · hunters · rudimentary potters · builders · healers · communal warriors.

Clans influence marriages, land distribution, **water use**, occupations, support for the chief, defense, funerary rites and food sharing.

### Government

Community chieftaincy decided at the Gathering Point by **in-person voting**.

**Institutions:** community chief · informal council of elders · circle of families · **guardian of the waters** · keeper of the food stores · observer of the seasons.

Decisions rest on experience, prestige, control of food, capacity to organize collective labor and family support.

### Religion

**None.** No temple, priesthood, patron deity, Faith or religious buildings. Burials are a social custom with no mechanical effect.

### Economy

Direct barter.

**Resources:** grain · rice or millet where applicable · dates and fruit · fish · meat · wool · clay · reeds · bamboo · timber · stone · leather · milk · herbs · fiber.

**Production:** stone tools · baskets · mats · nets · rudimentary pottery · simple textiles · **mud bricks** · reed or bamboo boats · dried food · herbal remedies.

### Buildings

**Housing:** reed hut · bamboo hut · mud house · family house · communal house · family courtyard · fishers' shelter · herders' shelter.

**Food:** cultivation field · garden · early paddy where applicable · fishing spot · simple pen · primitive granary · rudimentary orchard · food drying area.

**Production:** stone workshop · rudimentary pottery · weaving area · reed and bamboo workshop · brick yard · simple tannery · herb house · rudimentary shipyard · communal fire.

**Infrastructure:** **earth ditch** · shallow well · **rudimentary dike** · small bridge · dirt path · communal warehouse · water distribution point.

**Administration:** **Gathering Point** · chief's house · circle of elders · communal store · land marker.

**Defense:** palisade · mud barrier · watchpost · rudimentary tower · simple gate · warriors' shelter.

### Military Forces

Temporarily levied citizens. There is no standing army.

Tribal spearman · slinger · hunter · armed fisher · rudimentary archer · warrior with club · scout · defender with wooden shield.

### Cultural trait — Collective Labor of the Waters

The community can summon workers to dig and repair ditches, channels and dikes.

**Benefits:** greater agricultural production · better access to water · more cooperation between families · accelerated agricultural growth.

**Risks:** workers abandon other tasks · clans may refuse to participate · poorly planned ditches cause floods · disputes over water generate conflict between families.

### Stone Age Missions

1. **Found the community** — build dwellings, secure water, create a field or fishing spot.
2. **Raise the Gathering Point** — gather five houses close together, build the site and **choose the Descent**.
3. **Feed the families** — produce grain, raise animals, build a granary, avoid famine.
4. **Dig the first ditch** — plan the route, summon workers, bring water to the fields.
5. **Survive the flood** — reinforce dikes, protect houses, save food stores.
6. **Gather the clans** — secure family support, resolve a land dispute, organize collective labor.
7. **Protect the settlement** — build a palisade, create watchposts, organize defenders.
8. **Discover copper** — find ore, gather material, produce the first metal object.

### Unavailable Systems

Temple · patron deity · priesthood · Faith · writing · scribes · formal market · contracts · clan seat · clan missions · taxation · formal government · standing army · recorded laws · palace · bureaucracy · formal calendar.

### Advancing to the Copper Age

- Sustain a stable population.
- Secure food and water.
- Build a functional ditch or channel.
- Build a granary.
- Keep clans stable.
- Hold recognized leadership.
- Build a workshop.
- Discover copper and produce a metal tool.
- Create a permanent barter area.
- **Gathering Point built and Descent chosen.**
