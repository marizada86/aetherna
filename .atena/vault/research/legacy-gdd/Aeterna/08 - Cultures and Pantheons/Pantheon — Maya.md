# Copper Age — Single Target

## Chaac — Axe of the Rain

Rain, thunder, lightning, agriculture and fertility of the fields.

**City passive:** Agriculture and water storage +5% · wells and reservoirs produce additional water during rains · droughts cause less production loss.
*Style: rain and agricultural survival.*

**Attack — Axe of Thunder.** Lightning damage · applies shock · reduces speed · **interrupts abilities being cast** · metallic units take additional damage · on wet terrain the shock duration increases.

**Defense — Restoring Rain.** Restores health and vigor · removes burning · reduces fire damage · temporary regeneration · healing increased near a plantation or reservoir.

**Mobility — Step of the Storm.** Moves the target quickly · crosses mud, shallow water and flooded terrain · removes slow · lightning resistance on arrival · **the target cannot be knocked down during the movement**.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · does not transport siege engines · the destination must have valid space.

## Xbalanqué — Cunning of the Hero

One of the Hero Twins. Cunning, hunting, trials, combat against underworld forces and the overcoming of powerful enemies.

**City passive:** Hunters, explorers and light units +5% · missions against creatures or hidden threats offer better rewards · traps are more effective.
*Style: hunting, heroism and cunning.*

**Attack — Shot of the Hero Twin.** High damage · ignores part of evasion · additional damage against creatures, monsters and spiritual units · reveals the target · interrupts abilities being cast · **if the enemy is hidden or camouflaged, damage increases**.
*Trial effect:* **if the target has more maximum health than Xbalanqué**, the attack ignores part of the armor and the target temporarily loses spiritual resistance.

**Defense — Deception of the Hero Twins.** The first attack against the target **strikes an illusion** · increases evasion · **removes a pursuit mark** · grants resistance to fear · the attacker is revealed · if the illusion is destroyed, the ally recovers part of their vigor.

**Mobility — Leap Over Xibalba.** Repositions the target · crosses pits, traps and low obstacles · removes entrapment and immobilization · hidden during part of the journey · evasion bonus on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross deep walls · does not transport siege engines · cannot end in an occupied cell.

## Kinich Ahau — Face of the Sun

The Sun, daylight, authority and solar power.

**City passive:** Daytime production +5% · temples and squares gain additional Prestige · **workers recover morale at dawn**.
*Style: sun and religious authority.*

**Attack — Incandescent Gaze.** Luminous damage · applies burning · temporarily blinds · removes camouflage, invisibility and illusions · shadowy and spiritual creatures take additional damage.
*Daytime effect:* by day the damage increases, the range increases and blindness lasts longer.

**Defense — Warmth of the Living Sun.** Restores vigor · grants light regeneration · removes freezing · reduces cold and darkness damage · raises morale · **by day it grants additional resistance**.

**Mobility — Trail of the Sun.** Greatly increased speed · removes slow and disorientation · reveals traps along the route · **prevents ambushes** · additional vision on arrival.
*Daytime effect:* by day the range increases, the target gains additional accuracy and the path travelled stays illuminated for a few moments.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Kukulcán — Serpent of the Skies

The Feathered Serpent, wind, rain, knowledge, the sky and civilizational order.

**City passive:** Research, trade and travel speed +10% · roads and trade routes have greater range · ceremonial centers produce additional Prestige.
*Style: knowledge, wind and connection.*

**Attack — Breath of the Feathered Serpent** (up to 3×3, line/cone/square). Wind damage · pushes enemies back · reduces accuracy · disorganizes formations · **removes smoke, fog and gases** · flying units take additional damage · the epicenter may knock down light units.

**Defense — Spirals of the Sky** (up to 3×3, square or ring). Deflects projectiles · reduces ranged damage · **prevents the spread of gases** · increases evasion · maintains formation · enemies attempting to enter are pushed back.
*Additional effect:* priests, scholars and commanders in the area gain spiritual resistance, **recover their abilities faster** and extend the defense's duration.

**Mobility — Ascent of the Serpent** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies through the air · ignores terrain and units · crosses small obstacles and low walls · keeps relative positions · the landing pushes light enemies back · they arrive with vision and speed bonuses.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy siege engines are unaffected · anti-air barriers may prevent passage.

## Ixchel — Weaving of Life

The Moon, medicine, healing, fertility, childbirth, weaving and the waters.

**City passive:** Health, population growth and textile production +10% · healers produce more medicine · families recover faster from negative events.
*Style: medicine, fertility and craftsmanship.*

**Attack — Threads of the Moon** (up to 3×3, square or cross). Reduces speed · **immobilizes units at the center** · reduces accuracy · interrupts movement abilities · gradual spiritual damage · **enemies attempting to leave the area take additional damage**.
*Night effect:* at night the duration increases, spiritual damage is amplified and hidden units are revealed.

**Defense — Mantle of the Weaver** (up to 3×3, square). Gradual healing · removes poison, disease and bleeding · reduces spiritual damage · increases resistance to fear · the first negative effect applied to each ally is nullified · **units with low health receive additional healing**.
*Night effect:* at night regeneration increases, duration is extended and allies gain additional evasion.

**Mobility — Path of the Woven Moon** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports or guides allies to the destination · crosses rivers, pits and small obstacles · removes slow · maintains formation · they arrive with regeneration and evasion.
*Limitations:* does not cross great walls · does not transport heavy machines · **the path can be blocked by magical destruction effects**.

## Huracán — Heart of the Storm

Wind, storms, thunder, celestial fire and the destructive forces of nature.

**City passive:** Construction and reconstruction speed +10% · natural disasters cause less permanent damage · **storms may temporarily increase water and Faith production**.
*Style: storms, destruction and reconstruction.*

**Attack — Heart of the Storm** (up to 3×3, square or cone). Wind and lightning damage · pushes enemies back · reduces accuracy · disorganizes formations · applies shock · the epicenter takes additional damage · **light siege engines may be temporarily disabled**.
*Persistent effect:* the area remains unstable — units suffer reduced speed, projectiles have reduced accuracy and small fires may be extinguished or spread.

**Defense — Eye of the Hurricane** (up to 3×3, square or ring). Reduces ranged damage · deflects projectiles · prevents knockback · removes smoke and gases · increases evasion · enemies attempting to enter are slowed · **buildings gain resistance to storms**.

**Mobility — Current of the Hurricane** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies quickly · ignores difficult terrain and units · crosses small obstacles · the landing pushes enemies back · they arrive with speed and evasion bonuses · removes slow and entrapment.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy machines are unaffected · **the destination may vary slightly within the selected area**.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Itzamná — Wisdom of Creation

Creation, wisdom, writing, knowledge, the sky, order and divine authority.

**City passive:** Research, Faith and administration +15% · schools, temples and archives **share part of their bonuses** · the city can sustain one additional technology or decree.
*Style: creation, writing and knowledge.*

**Attack — The Word of Creation Undone** (up to 5×5, square). Spiritual damage · removes enemies' positive effects · silences abilities · reduces defense and magical resistance · **dispels summons** · **temporarily disables magical or ritual buildings** · the epicenter takes an additional penalty.
*Rewriting:* each enemy struck **temporarily loses their dominant attribute** — offensive units lose damage, defensive units lose armor, fast units lose speed, commanders lose leadership efficiency.

**Defense — Codex of the First Creation** (up to 5×5, square). Reduces physical and spiritual damage · nullifies the first negative effect applied to each ally · removes confusion, fear and curses · amplifies commander and priest bonuses · buildings gradually restore integrity · defensive abilities recharge faster.
*Adaptation of the codex:* the protection **adapts to the damage type most received** — physical raises armor, magical raises spiritual resistance, ranged raises evasion, elemental grants matching resistance.

**Mobility — Paths Written in the Sky** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports a large formation · ignores terrain, rivers and walls · keeps relative positions · **reveals the destination before arrival** · removes fear, confusion and disorganization · they arrive with vision and resistance bonuses · **the path remains available briefly for reinforcements**.
*Limitations:* the destination must be visible or under allied control · does not transport structures · equivalent divine barriers may prevent the opening.

## Ah Puch — Presence of Death

Death, decay, corpses, fear and the underworld.

**City passive:** Funerary administration and intimidation +15% · **nearby enemies suffer reduced morale** · epidemics and deaths produce less instability, provided there are enough cemeteries.
*Style: death, fear and control.*

**Attack — Putrefaction of Xibalba** (up to 5×5, square). Spiritual and gradual damage · reduces healing received · applies fear · reduces speed · **corpses in the area extend the duration** · defeated units become foci of decay · the epicenter may execute enemies with very low health.
*Contaminated terrain:* enemies lose vigor · healers work with lower efficiency · **enemy resurrections are prevented** · allied spiritual units gain bonuses.

**Defense — Procession of the Dead** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Resistance to fear · deaths cause less morale loss · **defeated units return as temporary animated corpses** that block attacks and paths · enemies cannot control allied corpses · **each summoned dead increases the area's spiritual resistance**.
*Limitations:* returned units cannot be healed · they vanish at the end · the quantity depends on existing bodies or units defeated during the activation.

**Mobility — Procession to Xibalba** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through Xibalba · ignores terrain, walls and buildings · they cannot be intercepted · enemies near the destination suffer fear · they arrive camouflaged in funerary mist · spiritual units recover vigor.
*Cost:* living units lose a small amount of morale and receive a temporary healing reduction — **priests and spiritual units are exempt**.

## Camazotz — Terror of the Cave

Bats, caves, night, sacrifice, blood and sudden death.

**City passive:** Underground production and night attacks +15% · mines have greater yield · night units and ambushes deal more damage · **districts without lighting lose Satisfaction faster**.
*Style: caves, night and fear.*

**Attack — Eclipse of the Bats** (up to 5×5, square or cone). Physical and spiritual damage · reduces vision · applies bleeding · reduces accuracy · causes fear · enemies with low health take additional damage · **the epicenter suffers concentrated attacks from the bats**.
*Night effect:* at night damage increases, duration grows, hidden units are revealed by the bats and **the swarm grants camouflage to allies**.

**Defense — Mantle of the Cave** (up to 5×5, square). Reduces the accuracy of external enemies · grants camouflage to allies · increases evasion · reduces projectile damage · **allies recover health by causing bleeding** · enemies entering suffer fear and vision loss · night units gain additional resistance.
*Weakness:* **solar effects and intense illumination reduce the protection** · the area can be partially revealed by light abilities.

**Mobility — Flight of the Night** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the air · ignores walls, terrain and units · they cannot be hit by melee attacks during the journey · they arrive camouflaged · the landing reduces enemy vision · **units attacking right after arrival apply bleeding**.
*Night effect:* at night the range increases, camouflage lasts longer and enemies near the destination suffer fear.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy siege engines are unaffected · **solar effects may interrupt or reduce the range**.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| Deity | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Chaac** — Rain Denied | Agriculture and water storage −10% · wells and reservoirs produce no extra water during rains · droughts cause amplified loss |
| **Xbalanqué** — Cunning Lost | Hunters, explorers and light units −10% · missions against hidden threats give worse rewards · **traps fail** |
| **Kinich Ahau** — Veiled Face | Daytime production −10% · temples and squares generate no Prestige · **workers lose morale at dawn** |
| **Kukulcán** — Serpent Fallen | Research, trade and travel speed −20% · **roads and trade routes lose range** · ceremonial centers generate no Prestige |
| **Ixchel** — Thread Broken | Health, population growth and textile production −20% · healers produce less medicine · families do not recover from negative events |
| **Huracán** — Storm Unleashed | Construction and reconstruction speed −20% · **natural disasters cause amplified permanent damage** · storms destroy instead of replenishing |
| **Itzamná** — Creation Undone | Research, Faith and administration −30% · schools, temples and archives stop sharing bonuses · **the city loses the additional technology or decree it sustained** |
| **Ah Puch** — Death Without Order | Funerary administration and intimidation −30% · **nearby enemies gain morale instead of losing it** · epidemics and deaths cause instability even with enough cemeteries |
| **Camazotz** — Sealed Cave | Underground production and night attacks −30% · mines lose yield · night units and ambushes deal less damage · **every district loses Satisfaction at night, lit or not** |

## Strategic Identity

**Chaac** lightning damage, healing and movement on wet terrain · **Xbalanqué** precision, deception, combat against creatures and escape from traps · **Kinich Ahau** solar damage, revelation and safe mobility during the day · **Kukulcán** wind control, defense against projectiles and aerial mobility · **Ixchel** healing, movement control and lunar protection · **Huracán** area damage, disorganization and fast repositioning · **Itzamná** ability denial, adaptive protection and strategic transport · **Ah Puch** death, decay, temporary units and crossing through the underworld · **Camazotz** bleeding, darkness, fear and aerial infiltration.
