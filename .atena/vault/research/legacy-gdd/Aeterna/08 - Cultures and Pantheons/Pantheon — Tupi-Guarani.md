# Pantheon — Tupi-Guarani

**Category:** [[08 - Cultures and Pantheons]] · **Culture:** [[Culture — Tupi-Guarani]]

> **Provisional entities.** The source marks **Cairé/Rairé** and **Itaguará** as interpretations built for the project, not canonical reconstructions. **Tau**, **Angatupyry** and **Anhangá** also carry the caveat "treated in the project as…". See the adaptation note in [[Culture — Tupi-Guarani]].

# Copper Age — Single Target

## Cairé / Rairé — Communal Celebration

*Provisional interpretation for the game setting.* Communal celebration, music, collective rituals, memory and unity among community members.

**City passive:** Faith and Satisfaction gained from festivals +5% · celebrations require 10% less food · **relations between clans recover faster after communal feasts**.
*Style: celebration, tradition and unity.*

**Attack — Song of Dishonor.** Reduces morale · reduces accuracy · interrupts abilities being cast · temporarily disables leadership bonuses · **if the target is a commander, their subordinates receive smaller bonuses**.
*Special effect:* if the target is **isolated**, the morale reduction increases and the weakening lasts longer.

**Defense — Song of the Ancestors.** Restores morale · removes fear · reduces damage taken · prevents involuntary retreat · resistance to confusion and manipulation.
*Clan effect:* if the target is near members of the **same clan**, the duration increases and they gain vigor regeneration.

**Mobility — Rhythm of the Journey.** Increases speed · reduces vigor consumption · removes disorientation · **prevents the target from losing morale during the movement** · passes through allied units.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not transport siege engines · **the effect ends if the target is immobilized**.

## Caipora / Curupira — Guardian of the Forest

Protection of the forests, animals, wild paths, hunting and punishment of abusive exploiters.

**City passive:** Hunting, gathering and forest exploitation +5% · forests recover resources faster · **excessive exploitation reduces or removes the bonus**.
*Style: forest, animals and environmental balance.*

**Attack — Inverted Path.** Applies disorientation · **temporarily inverts movement commands** · reduces accuracy · removes the ability to track units · the target may walk to a different cell than intended.
*Against exploiters:* hunters, woodcutters and forest invaders take an additional speed penalty and **temporarily lose terrain bonuses**.

**Defense — Protection of the Forest.** Grants camouflage · increases evasion · reduces projectile damage · **prevents tracking** · the first attack received deals reduced damage.
*In forest:* within dense vegetation, camouflage becomes stronger, the target recovers vigor and the duration increases.

**Mobility — Steps Backward.** Repositions the target to a valid cell · crosses dense forest and small obstacles · removes slow and pursuit · **the real route is hidden from enemies** · brief camouflage on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · does not transport machines · **shorter range in urban areas**.

## Guaraci — Light That Feeds Life

The Sun, light, warmth, life, day and guidance.

**City passive:** Daytime agricultural production +5% · homes and farms gain additional Satisfaction at dawn · penalties caused by cold are reduced.
*Style: sun, life and agriculture.*

**Attack — Ray of Guaraci.** Luminous damage · applies burning · temporarily blinds · removes camouflage and invisibility · shadowy and spiritual creatures take additional damage.
*Daytime effect:* by day the damage increases, the range is extended and blindness lasts longer.

**Defense — Warmth of Life.** Restores vigor · grants regeneration · removes freezing and fear · reduces cold and darkness damage · raises morale.
*Daytime effect:* by day regeneration increases, the target gains additional resistance and **shadow effects have reduced duration**.

**Mobility — Path of Dawn.** Greatly increased speed · removes slow and disorientation · reveals traps · **prevents ambushes** · additional vision on arrival.
*Daytime effect:* by day the range increases, the target gains additional accuracy and the path stays illuminated for a few moments.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Tau — Temptation and Ambition

*Treated in the project as* an entity tied to temptation, ambition, conflict, corruption and the pursuit of power.

**City passive:** Production and trade +10% · ambitious clans generate more wealth · **corruption, rivalries and crime also grow faster**.
*Style: economic power at high social risk.*

**Attack — Seed of Discord** (up to 3×3, square). Applies confusion · reduces morale · **enemies may attack nearby allies** · disables formation bonuses · reduces commander efficiency · units at the center take a greater penalty.
*Ambition awakened:* **the strongest units within the area** become more aggressive, take additional damage and are more likely to ignore orders.

**Defense — Pact of Ambition** (up to 3×3, square). Increases attack and defense · grants resistance to fear · **units with low health deal more damage** · the first attack suffered deals reduced damage · eliminating enemies restores vigor.
*Cost:* at the end of the ability, part of the morale is lost, **units that defeated no enemies receive exhaustion** and rival clans may gain discontent.

**Mobility — Shortcut of Temptation** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies between two areas · ignores low obstacles and units · attack bonus on arrival · removes immobilization · they arrive with increased speed.
*Risk:* each unit receives a **light random consequence** — morale loss, temporary defense reduction, additional attack gain or brief camouflage.

## Angatupyry — Path of Good

*Treated in the project as* an entity tied to goodness, conscience, cooperation, protection and resistance to corruption.

**City passive:** Order and Satisfaction +10% · helping clans, keeping agreements and completing peaceful missions grants Faith · corruption and sabotage have reduced impact.
*Style: cooperation, morality and stability.*

**Attack — Rejection of Evil** (up to 3×3, square or cross). Spiritual damage **against corrupted units** · **removes bonuses gained through fear, cruelty or corruption** · reduces damage dealt by enemies · prevents the execution of allies with low health · shadowy units take additional damage.
*Central effect:* the enemy at the center loses positive effects, has their next offensive ability cancelled and takes a morale penalty.

**Defense — Circle of Harmony** (up to 3×3, square). Reduces damage taken · restores morale · removes fear, confusion and corruption · **shares part of the damage among allies** · units cannot be controlled by enemies · **different clans receive greater cooperation**.
*Harmony:* **the more different allies stand within the area**, the greater the damage reduction and morale regeneration.

**Mobility — Path of the Good Choice** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Moves allies while keeping formation · reveals traps along the way · removes fear and disorientation · prevents ambushes · they arrive with a defense bonus · **cannot end in known dangerous cells**.
*Limitations:* does not cross great walls · **cannot be used to enter corrupted areas without vision** · heavy machines move more slowly.

## Itaguará — Guardian of the Wolf Stone

*Provisional entity based on the meaning attributed to the name within the project.* Stone, the wolf, territorial defense, resilience and vigilance.

**City passive:** Quarries, walls and defenders +10% · stone buildings cost 5% fewer resources · **warriors defending the city's territory gain a morale bonus**.
*Style: stone, territory and protection.*

**Attack — Fangs of Stone** (up to 3×3, line/cross/square). Physical damage · reduces speed · may immobilize units at the epicenter · damages armor and siege engines · **creates small temporary obstacles** · units pushed against the stones take additional damage.

**Defense — Pack of Stone** (up to 3×3, square). Increases armor · prevents knockback · reduces projectile damage · **adjacent units share part of the defense** · buildings gain additional resistance · enemies entering have reduced speed.
*Pack cohesion:* **the more allies remain close together**, the greater the defense and resistance to fear.

**Mobility — Trail of the Stone Wolf** (origin up to 3×3, path of up to 3 cells). Moves the formation through difficult terrain · crosses rocks and light natural obstacles · keeps relative positions · **projectile resistance during the journey** · they arrive with a defensive bonus.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · **the route must touch the ground** · does not transport structures.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Tupã — Voice of Thunder

Thunder, lightning, rain, the sky, creation and the manifestation of divine power.

**City passive:** Faith, water production and Military strength +15% · storms replenish reservoirs and temporarily strengthen warriors · **lightning may damage buildings without proper protection**.
*Style: thunder, rain and divine authority.*

**Attack — Voice of the Sky** (up to 5×5, square or cross). High lightning damage · the epicenter takes additional damage · applies shock · stuns directly struck units · metallic units take amplified damage · siege engines may be disabled · **the rain extinguishes fires**.
*Echoes of thunder:* each bolt may produce a sound wave that reduces morale, interrupts abilities and pushes light units back.

**Defense — Shield of the Storm** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Reduces ranged damage · deflects projectiles · extinguishes burns · increases lightning resistance · enemies entering suffer shock · buildings gain protection against fire · **allies recover vigor under the rain**.
*Thunder effect:* the first enemy to strike each ally receives a **returning bolt** and suffers reduced speed.

**Mobility — Leap of Thunder** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies instantly · ignores terrain, rivers and walls · **the destination receives an electrical discharge** · enemies are pushed back or stunned · they arrive with speed and attack bonuses · removes slow and fear.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures · heavy machines require more space at the destination · **insulating barriers may reduce the range**.

## Paranã — Greatness of the Waters

The great waters, rivers, the sea, abundance, navigation and the force of currents.

**City passive:** Fishing, navigation and river or maritime trade +15% · ports and coastal villages have greater storage capacity · aquatic expeditions suffer fewer losses.
*Style: waters, navigation and abundance.*

**Attack — Meeting of the Great Waters** (up to 5×5, line/cone/square). Physical damage · pushes enemies back · knocks down light units · extinguishes fires · damages siege engines · reduces speed · turns the terrain into a flooded area.
*Central current:* the central cells **drag units**, strongly reduce accuracy and **may split formations**.

**Defense — Shelter of the Waters** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Reduces physical and fire damage · removes burns · heals gradually · projectiles lose force · prevents charges · aquatic units gain greater regeneration · **buildings near water restore integrity**.

**Mobility — Path of the Great Rivers** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports troops and vessels · ignores rivers, lakes, flooded terrain and low obstacles · **temporarily crosses land areas** · they cannot be intercepted during part of the route · they arrive with vigor restored · the destination is temporarily flooded.
*Aquatic effect:* if origin and destination are linked by water, the range increases, movement is faster and **heavy vessels may also be transported**.

## Anhangá — Lord of the Shadowed Paths

*Associated, within the project,* with the spirits of the forest, protection of animals, fear, haunting and punishment of invaders.

**City passive:** Forest defense and enemy intimidation +15% · invaders in wild territory suffer reduced morale and speed · **hunters who over-exploit the forest lose productivity and Satisfaction**.
*Style: spirits, forest, fear and punishment.*

**Attack — Hunt of the Spirits** (up to 5×5, square or cone). Spiritual damage · applies fear · reduces vision and speed · **reveals hunters, invaders and hidden units** · **units that have destroyed forests take additional damage** · spirits pursue enemies attempting to flee.
*In forest:* within woodland the damage increases, the area stays haunted longer and enemies continuously lose morale.

**Defense — Sanctuary of the Animals** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Grants camouflage to allies · increases evasion · reduces projectile damage · animals and spiritual units gain regeneration · enemies entering suffer fear · prevents tracking · **disables hunting traps**.
*Environmental protection:* within the area, **trees and natural resources cannot be destroyed**, enemies take an exploitation penalty and allies recover vigor.

**Mobility — Crossing of the Haunted Forest** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies **between areas of forest or wild territory** · ignores vegetation, units and natural obstacles · camouflage during the journey · enemies near the destination suffer fear · they arrive with evasion and speed bonuses · spiritual units recover vigor.
*Limitations:* **shorter range in cities or completely devastated terrain** · does not transport heavy siege engines · solar abilities may reveal the route.

## Wrath of the Abandoned Entity

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| Entity | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Cairé/Rairé** — Song Lost | Faith and Satisfaction from festivals −10% · celebrations require 20% more food · **relations between clans do not recover after communal feasts** |
| **Caipora/Curupira** — Vengeful Forest | Hunting, gathering and forest exploitation −10% · forests do not recover resources · **even moderate exploitation depletes the territory** |
| **Guaraci** — Sun Withdrawn | Daytime agricultural production −10% · homes and farms gain no Satisfaction at dawn · **cold penalties double** |
| **Tau** — Ambition Betrayed | Production and trade −20% · ambitious clans stop generating wealth · **corruption, rivalries and crime keep growing at double rate** |
| **Angatupyry** — Conscience Absent | Order and Satisfaction −20% · helping clans, keeping agreements and completing peaceful missions grants no Faith · corruption and sabotage have amplified impact |
| **Itaguará** — Cracked Stone | Quarries, walls and defenders −20% · stone buildings cost 10% more resources · **warriors lose morale defending their own territory** |
| **Tupã** — Thunder Turned | Faith, water production and Military strength −30% · storms neither replenish reservoirs nor strengthen warriors · **lightning strikes buildings even when protected** |
| **Paranã** — Closed Waters | Fishing, navigation and river or maritime trade −30% · ports and coastal villages lose storage capacity · aquatic expeditions suffer amplified losses |
| **Anhangá** — Hostile Forest | Forest defense and intimidation −30% · **invaders in wild territory suffer no penalty at all** · the city's own hunters lose productivity and Satisfaction |

## Strategic Identity

**Cairé/Rairé** morale, leadership, collective memory and organized marches · **Caipora/Curupira** confusion, camouflage, forests and punishment of exploiters · **Guaraci** solar damage, revelation and safe mobility during the day · **Tau** offensive power, risk, corruption and unpredictable choices · **Angatupyry** purification, cooperation, stability and collective protection · **Itaguará** stone, resilience, territorial control and compact formations · **Tupã** lightning damage, counterattack and instant mobility · **Paranã** water, terrain control, protection and mass transport · **Anhangá** fear, spirits, environmental defense and forest infiltration.
