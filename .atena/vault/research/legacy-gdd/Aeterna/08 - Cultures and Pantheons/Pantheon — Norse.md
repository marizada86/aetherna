# Copper Age — Single Target

## Freyr — Lord of the Bountiful Harvest

Fertility, prosperity, harvests, peace and abundance.

**City passive:** Food production +5% · farms and pens produce +1 additional food when operating at full capacity.
*Style: population growth and agriculture.*

**Attack — Ensnaring Root.** Sacred roots wrap a single enemy. Light initial damage · immobilizes · reduces defense while held · ranged units deal additional damage to the target.

**Defense — Gift of the Harvest.** Restores health and vigor · removes hunger and exhaustion · grants temporary regeneration.

**Mobility — Step of Spring.** Moves the target to a cell within range · ignores vegetation and farmland penalties · removes slow · brief speed boost on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls, does not transport siege engines.

## Uller — Paths of the Hunter

Hunting, the bow, winter, skis and mastery of wild terrain.

**City passive:** Hunters and fishers produce +5% · hunting posts suffer 20% less distance penalty from the city.
*Style: exploration and use of wild territories.*

**Attack — Arrow of Winter.** High damage · ignores part of armor · applies freezing · reduces speed · **if the target is already wounded, it may be immobilized**.

**Defense — Hunter's Cloak.** Makes the target hard to detect · increases evasion against projectiles · reduces movement noise · the first attack received deals reduced damage.
*Breaks on:* attacking · using an offensive ability · coming too close to an enemy detector.

**Mobility — Skis of Uller.** Greatly increased speed · ignores snow, ice, mud and forest penalties · crosses small slopes · prevents the target from being slowed during the effect.

## Bragi — Memory of the Skalds

Poetry, eloquence, music, memory and the preservation of heroic deeds.

**City passive:** Faith and Prestige production +5% · festivals cost 10% fewer resources.
*Style: culture, religion and social stability.*

**Attack — Verse of Dishonor.** Reduces morale · reduces accuracy · reduces leadership efficiency · **if the target is a commander, their bonuses to allies are temporarily disabled**.

**Defense — Saga of the Hero.** Raises morale · grants resistance to fear · reduces damage taken · prevents involuntary retreat · **if the target survives to the end, they gain Prestige or experience**.

**Mobility — Rhythm of the Journey.** Increases speed · reduces vigor consumption · prevents interruption by fear · lets the target hold a defensive stance while moving.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Thor — Protector of Midgard

Thunder, strength, protection, storms and combat against threats.

**City passive:** Urban defense +10% · buildings struck by disasters or attacks are repaired 15% faster.
*Style: defense and resilience.*

**Attack — Impact of Mjölnir** (3×3, square, epicenter at the central cell). High damage at the center · moderate damage in the other cells · stuns enemies at the epicenter · applies shock to the rest · damages buildings and siege engines · metallic units take additional damage.

**Defense — Wall of Thunder** (up to 3×3, line or square). Reduces damage taken by allies · blocks or reduces projectiles · enemies entering take shock · **enemy charges are interrupted** · buildings in the area gain additional resistance.

**Mobility — Leap of the Storm** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports all valid allied units · crosses low obstacles · the landing pushes enemies back · enemies in the central cell are stunned · allies arrive with a temporary speed bonus.
*Limitations:* does not transport structures; heavy siege engines are unaffected.

## Tyr — Unbreakable Oath

Justice, oaths, law, courage and sacrifice in battle.

**City passive:** Order +10% · warriors gain +5% Military strength **as long as the city has not broken treaties or clan promises**.
*Style: laws, discipline and honorable war.*

**Attack — Judgment of Oaths** (3×3, square). Reduces enemy defense · removes bonuses received from commanders · prevents invisibility and camouflage · enemies who attack first take additional damage · defeating a marked enemy raises allied morale.

**Defense — Unbreakable Oath** (3×3, square). Grants immunity to fear · increases physical resistance · prevents knockback · units cannot retreat involuntarily · **the fewer units remain in the area, the greater their resistance**.
*Restriction:* units lose part of the bonus by voluntarily leaving the area.

**Mobility — Advance of the Just Formation** (3×3, square). Moves units in formation keeping relative positions · reduces damage taken during movement · prevents knockback and disorganization · allies may pass through allied units during the advance.

## Loki — Unforeseen Advantage

Cunning, transformation, deception, unpredictability and the breaking of rules.

**City passive:** Trade +10% · on concluding a negotiation, a chance to receive an additional resource · **negative events caused by this passive may temporarily reduce Order**.
*Style: risk, trade and adaptation.*

**Attack — Army of Lies** (3×3, square). Creates illusory duplicates of the present units · the illusions mimic attacks · enemies may target them · when destroyed they apply confusion · the central unit generates a more resilient illusion.

**Defense — The Trickster's Swap** (3×3, square). The first attack received by each ally strikes an illusion · the ally is repositioned to a nearby cell · the illusion explodes on vanishing, dealing light damage and reducing enemy accuracy.

**Mobility — Impossible Shortcut** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allied units · ignores obstacles and small walls · units arrive camouflaged briefly · **the real destination may vary by one cell from the chosen point**.
*Risk:* small chance a unit appears in a different adjacent cell. Units are never sent to invalid cells.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Odin — Wisdom of the Allfather

Wisdom, war, death, runes, prophecy, magic and leadership.

**City passive:** Research and leader experience +15% · once per cycle, the city may reveal a future mission, threat or event in advance · libraries and knowledge centers produce +10% Prestige.
*Style: knowledge, planning and leadership.*

**Attack — Gungnir, the Inevitable Spear** (up to 5×5, line of up to 5 cells). Very high damage · pierces every unit in the line · ignores part of armor · **marks survivors with the Rune of Death** · marked targets take additional damage from all sources · if a marked target dies, the mark spreads to an adjacent enemy within the original area.

**Defense — Vision of Fate** (5×5, square). Reveals hidden units and traps · increases ally evasion · reduces the first damage taken by each ally · prevents enemy critical hits · allied commanders cannot be caught by surprise.
*Special effect:* the player sees indicators of the areas enemy abilities will strike **before** they activate.

**Mobility — Ride of Sleipnir** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports all valid allied units · ignores terrain, rivers, walls and elevation · they cannot be intercepted while moving · allies arrive with morale and vision bonuses · **commanders arrive with abilities partially recharged**.

## Freya — Lady of Fertility and Seiðr

Love, fertility, seiðr magic, war and the choosing of the slain.

**City passive:** Population growth and Satisfaction +15% · priests and seers raise the productivity of the district they work in by 10% · deaths in battle cause less Satisfaction loss.
*Style: population, magic and social cohesion.*

**Attack — Storm of Seiðr** (5×5, square). Spiritual damage · reduces accuracy · applies confusion · **enemies may attack nearby allies** · units at the center suffer more intense control · enemies who die during the effect grant additional devotion.

**Defense — Choice of the Valkyries** (5×5, square). Allies who would take fatal damage remain at minimum health · protected units cannot die for a few seconds · at the end they recover part of their health · saved warriors gain a large morale bonus · units not fatally wounded gain regeneration.
*Limitation:* each unit can avoid only one death per activation.

**Mobility — Falcon Cloak** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the air · ignores walls and terrain · they cannot be attacked during part of the journey · they arrive camouflaged · after landing they gain speed and evasion bonuses.
*Limitation:* units cannot attack while moving.

## Balder — Unshakable Light

Light, beauty, purity, peace and invulnerability.

**City passive:** Satisfaction and Order +15% · the first building that would be destroyed during each attack remains with a small amount of integrity · periods of peace gradually raise the city's Prestige.
*Style: prosperity, peace and preservation.*

**Attack — Judgment of the Immaculate Light** (5×5, square). Luminous damage · blinds enemies · removes camouflage, invisibility and illusions · shadowy creatures take additional damage · the epicenter deals greater damage · surviving enemies deal less damage for a period.

**Defense — Invulnerability of Balder** (5×5, square). Drastically reduces damage taken · **buildings cannot be destroyed during the effect** · units cannot be executed · removes burns, poisons and bleeding · the first attack against each ally is completely nullified.
*Optional thematic weakness:* a rare damage category or specific material may bypass part of the protection.

**Mobility — Road of Dawn** (corridor within 5×5, line or path of up to 5 cells). Allies move quickly along the road · removes fear, slow and disorientation · units cannot be blocked by enemies · the road reveals traps and hidden units · **reinforcements may use the path while it remains active**.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| God | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Freyr** — Hunger of Freyr | Food production −10% · farms and pens at full capacity produce 1 less · animals stop breeding |
| **Uller** — Empty Wilds | Hunters and fishers −10% · hunting posts suffer double distance penalty |
| **Bragi** — Silence of the Skalds | Faith and Prestige −10% · festivals cost 20% more · **no saga can be recorded** |
| **Thor** — Thunder Turned | Urban defense −20% · buildings struck by disasters or attacks take 30% longer to repair |
| **Tyr** — Broken Oath | Order −20% · **all of the city's treaties and clan promises are considered broken** · warriors lose 10% Military strength |
| **Loki** — The Trickster's Misfortune | Trade −20% · every concluded negotiation risks **losing** a resource · cascading random negative events |
| **Odin** — Veil of the Allfather | Research and leader experience −30% · no future event can be revealed · libraries and knowledge centers stop generating Prestige |
| **Freya** — Closed Womb | Population growth and Satisfaction −30% · priests and seers stop improving their districts · deaths in battle cause double Satisfaction loss |
| **Balder** — Light Extinguished | Satisfaction and Order −30% · **the first building struck in each attack is destroyed outright** · periods of peace stop generating Prestige |

## Strategic Identity

**Freyr** sustain, regeneration and natural control · **Uller** precision, stealth and terrain mastery · **Bragi** morale, leadership and empowering key units · **Thor** direct damage, area defense and aggressive entries · **Tyr** formations, discipline, laws and resilience · **Loki** deception, disorganization and unpredictable repositioning · **Odin** planning, target marking and strategic movement · **Freya** manipulation, survival and infiltration · **Balder** extreme protection, revelation and safe advance.
