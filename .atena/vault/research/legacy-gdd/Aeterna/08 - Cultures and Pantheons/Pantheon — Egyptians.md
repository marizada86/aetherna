# Copper Age — Single Target

## Osiris — Renewal of the Lands

Fertility, agriculture, renewal, death, resurrection and rule over the world of the dead.

**City passive:** Agricultural production +5% · fields recover fertility faster · cemeteries generate a small amount of Faith.
*Style: agriculture, death and renewal.*

**Attack — Judgment of the Return.** Spiritual damage · reduces healing received · temporarily reduces regeneration · applies the mark **Weight of Death** · **if the target takes fatal damage while marked, it cannot be revived or turned into a spiritual unit**.
*Additional effect:* the more wounded the target, the greater the initial damage and the longer the healing reduction lasts.

**Defense — Renewal of Osiris.** Gradually restores health · removes bleeding and disease · restores vigor · temporary resistance to spiritual damage · if it takes fatal damage, it remains at minimum health and the regeneration ends.
*In fertile areas:* near farms, canals or vegetation, healing is amplified and regeneration lasts longer.

**Mobility — Passage Through the Duat.** Repositions the target in a valid cell · passes through units, low obstacles and difficult terrain · **becomes intangible during part of the journey** · removes immobilization · spiritual camouflage on arrival.
*Cost:* the target loses a small amount of morale; spiritual units are exempt.
*Limitations:* does not cross deep walls · does not transport siege engines · cannot end on invalid terrain.

## Isis — Protection of the Family

Magic, healing, motherhood, protection, kingship and the restoration of Osiris.

**City passive:** Population growth and Health +5% · families affected by deaths or disasters recover Satisfaction faster · healers gain additional efficiency.
*Style: family, healing and protection.*

**Attack — Knot of Isis.** Immobilizes the target · reduces magical resistance · interrupts abilities being cast · **removes one positive effect** · gradual spiritual damage.
*Special effect:* if the enemy attempts to use an ability while bound, it is cancelled, the target takes additional damage and the immobilization increases slightly.

**Defense — Wings of Isis.** Reduces physical and spiritual damage · removes poison and disease · restores health · resistance to fear · **the first attack that would deal fatal damage is nullified**.
*Maternal protection:* if the target is a healer, priest, leader or young unit, the duration increases and the initial healing is amplified.

**Mobility — Flight of the Great Sorceress.** Transports the target through the air · ignores terrain, units and low obstacles · crosses small walls · cannot be hit by melee attacks during the journey · evasion and magical resistance on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not transport siege engines · the destination must have valid space · **anti-air effects can still strike the target during the movement**.

## Sobek — Strength of the River

The Nile, crocodiles, strength, fertility, military protection and predatory power.

**City passive:** Fishing and water production +5% · units near rivers gain a small defensive bonus · attacks by aquatic animals are less frequent.
*Style: rivers, strength and survival.*

**Attack — Bite of the Divine Crocodile.** High physical damage · applies bleeding · reduces speed · **prevents the target from retreating for a short period** · units in or near water take additional damage.
*Predatory effect:* if the target has low health, the damage is amplified and Sobek recovers part of the cooldown.

**Defense — Hide of Sobek.** Strongly increases armor · reduces projectile damage · resistance to bleeding · prevents knockback · **increased regeneration while the target is in water**.
*Counterattack:* the first enemy to attack in melee takes physical damage and suffers a temporary speed reduction.

**Mobility — Current of the Nile.** Moves the target quickly · **crosses rivers, canals and flooded areas** · removes slow · restores part of vigor · attack speed on arrival.
*Limitations:* land movement has reduced range · does not cross walls · does not transport heavy machines.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Thoth — Sacred Writing

Writing, wisdom, knowledge, the Moon, magic, measurement and divine records.

**City passive:** Research and scribe production +10% · archives increase administrative efficiency · laws and technologies cost less Knowledge.
*Style: writing, science and administration.*

**Attack — The Word That Undoes** (up to 3×3, square or cross). Moderate spiritual damage · **silences abilities** · removes one positive effect from each enemy · reduces accuracy · reduces magical resistance · reveals hidden units.
*Central hieroglyph:* the unit at the center has its next ability cancelled, temporarily loses leadership bonuses and takes additional damage when attempting to use magic.

**Defense — Record of Truth** (up to 3×3, square). Increases magical resistance · prevents confusion and mind control · reveals traps and illusions · the first negative effect applied to each ally is nullified · allied defensive abilities recharge faster.
*Damage record:* part of the damage taken is recorded and **a percentage is restored when the effect ends**.

**Mobility — Hieroglyphs of Transposition** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports units between the two areas keeping relative positions · removes confusion and disorganization · reveals the destination area · magical resistance on arrival · **can swap the positions of two allied formations**.
*Limitations:* the destination must be visible · does not transport structures · heavy siege engines take more space.

## Sekhmet — Ardor of the Lioness

War, destruction, solar fire, epidemics, healing and leonine ferocity.

**City passive:** Military strength +10% · houses of healing recover soldiers 15% faster · troops that win a battle gain temporary morale.
*Style: war and healing.*

**Attack — Fury of the Solar Lioness** (up to 3×3, square). Greatly increases damage · increases attack speed · applies burning to attacks · grants resistance to fear · **defeated enemies restore allies' vigor** · units at the center receive the greatest offensive bonus.
*Risk:* the defense of affected units is reduced · allies may pursue enemies beyond their original position.

**Defense — Healing Breath of Sekhmet** (up to 3×3, square). Heals allies · removes diseases and poisons · reduces burns and bleeding · temporary resistance to epidemics · warriors recover morale.
*Battle effect:* **the more enemies are defeated in the area during the effect, the greater the regeneration and the allies' resistance**.

**Mobility — Leap of the Lioness** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Moves allies quickly to the destination · the landing deals damage · pushes enemies back · the formation gains an attack bonus · light enemy units may be knocked down · allies cannot be slowed during the journey.
*Restriction:* a relatively direct trajectory · does not cross great walls · heavy machines are unaffected.

## Anubis — Guardian of the Necropolises

Mummification, necropolises, protection of the dead, passage of souls and the weighing of the heart.

**City passive:** Funerary order and Health +10% · corpses are treated faster · necropolises produce Faith and reduce epidemic risk.
*Style: funerals, preservation and the passage of the dead.*

**Attack — Weighing of the Heart** (up to 3×3, square). Spiritual damage · reduces morale · applies the mark **Heavy Heart** · marked units take additional damage if they attack protected allies · **badly wounded units may be executed** · undead and hostile spirits take amplified damage.
*Central judgment:* the unit at the center has its defense reduced, **cannot be revived if defeated** and loses leadership bonuses.

**Defense — Protection of the Necropolis** (up to 3×3, square). Reduces spiritual damage · prevents fear · removes diseases caused by corpses · **defeated units cannot be converted by enemies** · allies with low health gain additional resistance · allied bodies are preserved for possible later recovery.
*Special effect:* the first unit to die within the area becomes a protective shade that blocks attacks and vanishes at the end.

**Mobility — Path of the Embalmer** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies in spiritual form · passes through units, tombs and low obstacles · removes fear and disorientation · enemies near the destination are revealed · spiritual resistance on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not transport heavy siege engines · does not cross equivalent sacred barriers · **units cannot attack during the movement**.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Ra / Amun-Ra — Power of the Solar Disk

The Sun, creation, sovereignty, cosmic order and daily renewal.

**City passive:** Daytime production and Faith +15% · solar temples increase the productivity of nearby districts · the capital gains additional Prestige as it grows.
*Style: sun, sovereignty and prosperity.*

**Attack — Incandescent Eye of Ra** (up to 5×5, square/line/cone). Luminous and fire damage · very high damage at the epicenter · applies burning · blinds enemies · removes camouflage, invisibility and illusions · shadowy and spiritual creatures take additional damage · **solar fires linger for a period**.
*Daytime intensity:* by day the damage increases, blindness lasts longer and the area remains illuminated.

**Defense — Solar Barque** (up to 5×5, square or line). Strongly reduces spiritual and darkness damage · grants regeneration · removes fear, curses and shadow effects · raises morale · **prevents allied units from being turned into undead** · buildings gain protection against enemy fire and corruption.
*Solar cycle:* two phases — **Ascent** heals and removes negative effects · **Zenith** increases defense, accuracy and resistance.

**Mobility — Journey of the Celestial Barque** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the sky · ignores walls, rivers and elevation · illuminates the route · reveals hidden enemies and traps · they arrive with vision, accuracy and morale bonuses · **the luminous path remains briefly to guide reinforcements**.
*Temporal condition:* by day the range increases and the arrival causes a burst of light that blinds enemies.

## Horus — Eye of the Kingdom

Sky, kingship, protection, war, legitimacy and vision through the Eye of Horus.

**City passive:** Urban defense, vision and authority +15% · watchtowers have greater range · generals and rulers gain additional experience · **usurpers and saboteurs are detected earlier**.
*Style: royalty, protection and vigilance.*

**Attack — Avenging Eye of Horus** (up to 5×5, square). Reveals all enemies · removes camouflage and illusions · reduces evasion and armor · allied attacks gain accuracy and additional damage · **commanders and usurpers receive a stronger mark** · units at the epicenter may be blinded.
*Vengeance effect:* if a marked enemy eliminates an ally, it takes spiritual damage, becomes highlighted to all allied units and loses speed.

**Defense — Throne of the Falcon** (up to 5×5, square). Increases defense and accuracy · amplifies commander bonuses · grants resistance to fear and confusion · reveals threats entering the area · **prevents instant assassinations against leaders** · administrative buildings gain additional protection.
*Legitimacy:* the greater the loyalty of clans or troops, the greater the defensive bonus and the harder it is to break the formation.

**Mobility — Flight of the Royal Falcon** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the air · ignores terrain and walls · grants vision during the journey · **allows choosing the arrival positioning** · enemies near the destination are revealed · they arrive with accuracy and evasion bonuses.
*Royal effect:* transported commanders recover part of their abilities and grant an immediate morale bonus to nearby units.

## Set — Lord of the Desert and Chaos

Desert, storms, violence, disorder, strength, foreigners and resistance against chaotic threats.

**City passive:** Expeditions in deserts and hostile terrain +15% · troops suffer less attrition outside the city · **chaotic events grant opportunities but may reduce Order**.
*Style: desert, conflict and adaptation.*

**Attack — Red Storm** (up to 5×5, square or cone). Gradual physical damage · reduces vision and accuracy · applies disorientation · pushes light units back · damages siege engines · **creates sandy terrain that reduces speed** · the epicenter may hurl enemies into adjacent cells.
*Chaos of the storm:* at intervals, a **random cell** within the area receives a stronger gust, additional damage, knockback and brief blindness.

**Defense — Strength of the Adversary** (up to 5×5, square). Increases physical resistance · resistance to fear · reduces damage from creatures, monsters and chaotic forces · **allies with low health deal more damage** · prevents knockback · surrounded units gain additional protection.
*Power through adversity:* **the more enemies there are inside or around the area, the greater the defense, attack speed and resistance to control**.

**Mobility — Path of the Desert Storm** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies wrapped in sand · ignores difficult terrain, dunes and ruined areas · drastically reduces the chance of detection · enemies cannot intercept the route · the destination is covered by a temporary storm · they arrive with additional speed and resistance.
*Controlled risk:* units may appear in slightly different positions within the destination area · none is placed in an invalid cell · **rigid formations may arrive disorganized**.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| God | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Osiris** — Barren Land | Agricultural production −10% · **fields do not recover fertility** · cemeteries stop generating Faith |
| **Isis** — Protection Withdrawn | Population growth and Health −10% · families struck by deaths or disasters do not recover Satisfaction · healers lose efficiency |
| **Sobek** — Hostile River | Fishing and water production −10% · units near rivers lose the defensive bonus · **attacks by aquatic animals become frequent** |
| **Thoth** — Word Erased | Research and scribe production −20% · archives stop improving administration · laws and technologies cost more Knowledge |
| **Sekhmet** — Ardor Extinguished | Military strength −20% · houses of healing recover soldiers 30% slower · victories grant no morale to troops |
| **Anubis** — Desecrated Necropolis | Funerary order and Health −20% · **corpses pile up untreated** · necropolises stop generating Faith and begin raising epidemic risk |
| **Ra/Amun-Ra** — Disk Extinguished | Daytime production and Faith −30% · **solar temples reduce the productivity of nearby districts** · the capital loses Prestige as it grows |
| **Horus** — Eye Closed | Urban defense, vision and authority −30% · watchtowers lose range · generals and rulers stop gaining experience · **usurpers and saboteurs act undetected** |
| **Set** — Desert Turned | Expeditions in deserts and hostile terrain −30% · troops suffer amplified attrition outside the city · **chaotic events produce only consequences, never opportunities** |

## Strategic Identity

**Osiris** regeneration, control of death and spiritual crossings · **Isis** magic, healing, protection and individual aerial mobility · **Sobek** physical strength, resilience and aquatic movement · **Thoth** ability denial, information and precise repositioning · **Sekhmet** aggressive combat, healing during battle and charges · **Anubis** judgment, protection of the dead and spiritual movement · **Ra/Amun-Ra** solar damage, purification and strategic movement through the sky · **Horus** vision, leadership, legitimacy and organized aerial movement · **Set** terrain control, resilience under pressure and unpredictable mobility.
