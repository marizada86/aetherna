# Copper Age — Single Target

## Shennong — Teachings of Agriculture

Agriculture, medicinal herbs, cultivation, food and the discovery of plant properties.

**City passive:** Farm and medicinal herb production +5% · agricultural workers suffer less from disease · new crops are researched 10% faster.
*Style: agriculture and natural medicine.*

**Attack — Poison of the Hundred Herbs.** Gradual damage · reduces healing received · reduces vigor recovery · weakens resistance to disease and poison · **additional damage if the target is already poisoned**.

**Defense — Elixir of the Divine Farmer.** Restores health · removes poison and disease · temporary resistance to new negative effects · increases vigor regeneration.
*Special effect:* workers, farmers and healers receive amplified healing.

**Mobility — Step Between the Plantations.** Moves the target to a valid cell · ignores field, crop and low-vegetation penalties · removes terrain slow · restores part of vigor on arrival · temporary resistance to natural poisons.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not cross large bodies of water · does not transport siege engines.

## Chang'e and Houyi — Balance of Moon and Bow

Chang'e: the Moon, immortality and celestial ascent. Houyi: the bow, hunting and the feat of shooting down the excess suns.

**City passive:** Hunters produce +5% **during the day** · Faith and Satisfaction +5% **at night** · watchposts have greater range.
*Style: balance, hunting and celestial cycles.*

**Attack — Arrow That Fells the Sun.** Very high damage · great range · ignores part of armor · **removes bonuses related to the Sun, fire or light** · additional damage if the target is flying, mounted or on high ground.
*Special effect:* if the arrow eliminates the target, Houyi recovers part of the ability's cooldown.

**Defense — Lunar Veil of Chang'e.** Increases evasion · reduces ranged damage · grants gradual regeneration · removes burning · reduces the chance of detection.
*Night effect:* at night regeneration is amplified, the target gains partial camouflage and the duration increases.

**Mobility — Ascent to the Lunar Palace.** Transports the target through the air · ignores low obstacles, units and terrain · **crosses small walls** · the target cannot be struck during part of the journey · slow fall and evasion on arrival.
*Night effect:* at night the range increases.
*Limitations:* does not transport siege engines or structures · the destination must have a valid space.

## Xihe and Taiyang Shen — Course of the Sun

Xihe: the movement of the suns and the driving of the solar chariot. Taiyang Shen: the solar deity and the power of daylight.

**City passive:** Work speed during the day +5% · farms and solar buildings produce an additional amount · penalties caused by cold are reduced.
*Style: productivity and solar energy.*

**Attack — Concentrated Ray of the Sun.** Luminous damage · applies burning · temporarily blinds · removes camouflage and invisibility · additional damage against shadowy, spiritual or nocturnal creatures.
*Daylight effect:* by day the damage increases, blindness lasts longer and the range is extended.

**Defense — Warmth of Life.** Restores vigor · reduces cold damage · removes freezing · temporarily increases resistance · light regeneration.
*Daylight effect:* by day the target also gains attack speed, resistance to fear and greater vigor recovery.

**Mobility — Solar Chariot.** Moves quickly in a straight line · removes slow · prevents further speed reductions during the route · illuminates the path travelled · reveals traps and hidden units near the route.
*Daylight effect:* by day the movement has greater range.
*Limitations:* the target cannot change direction while moving · large obstacles block the route.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Chiyou — Lord of Weapons

War, metallurgy, weapons, martial strength and violent confrontation.

**City passive:** Weapon production and Military strength +10% · newly recruited troops start with additional experience · **army upkeep rises slightly**.
*Style: militarization.*

**Attack — Mist of War** (up to 3×3, square).
*On enemies:* reduces vision · reduces accuracy · disables formation bonuses · hinders receiving orders from commanders · increases damage taken from melee units.
*On allies:* grants resistance to fear · increases attack speed · **allows seeing normally within the mist**.

**Defense — Body of Bronze** (up to 3×3, square). Strongly increases armor · reduces projectile damage · prevents knockback · grants resistance to bleeding · reduces damage taken from common weapons.
*Penalty:* movement speed is reduced · **affected units take greater lightning damage**.

**Mobility — March of the Eighty Brothers** (origin up to 3×3, path of up to 3 cells). Moves all allied units in the area forward · pushes enemies back · breaks formations · destroys light barricades · keeps relative positions · grants resistance to control during the advance.
*Restriction:* fixed direction · does not cross complete walls.

## Jiutian Xuannü — Celestial Strategy

Military strategy, esoteric knowledge, protection, tactics and celestial teachings.

**City passive:** Military research and defensive efficiency +10% · the player receives additional information about enemy armies · troops prepared before a battle receive a temporary bonus.
*Style: strategy and military intelligence.*

**Attack — Formation of the Nine Stars** (up to 3×3, square). Marks all enemies in the area · reduces evasion and armor · removes camouflage · allied attacks against marked targets gain accuracy · the central target takes an additional defense penalty.
*Coordinated effect:* **if three or more allies attack the same area, they deal additional damage**.

**Defense — Diagram of the Nine Layers** (up to 3×3, square). Three defensive layers: outer reduces ranged damage · middle reduces melee damage · inner reduces spiritual or magical damage. **Each attack partially destroys one of the layers.**
*Additional effect:* allies keep formation · units cannot be pushed back while at least one layer remains active.

**Mobility — Steps of the Nine Stars** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports or repositions keeping relative positions · **reveals the destination area before the movement** · removes disorganization · accuracy bonus on arrival · **may perform up to three small displacements in sequence**.
*Limitations:* total distance remains limited by the ult's range · does not cross magical barriers · heavy machines perform only one displacement.

## Zao Jun — Guardian of the Hearth

Household fire, the home, families, the kitchen and observation of the inhabitants' conduct.

**City passive:** Residential Satisfaction +10% · houses consume 10% less fuel · districts with sufficient food produce additional Faith.
*Style: housing, families and stability.*

**Attack — Judgment of the Household Flames** (up to 3×3, square or cross). Fire damage · applies burning · **damages supplies** · reduces vigor regeneration · **enemies who committed crimes, looting or destruction of homes take additional damage**.
*Terrain effect:* the cells stay heated for a short period and deal light damage to enemies.

**Defense — Inviolable Home** (up to 3×3, square). Reduces damage taken · prevents fires · removes burns · slowly restores allies' health · raises morale · units cannot be driven out by fear.
*On buildings:* homes, kitchens and warehouses in the area gain additional resistance, restore integrity and **cannot be set on fire during the effect**.

**Mobility — Return Home** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports allies to a home, kitchen, household temple or urban seat · removes fear · **removes pursuit and enemy marks** · restores part of vigor · regeneration on arrival.
*Limitations:* the destination must be under allied control · does not transport siege engines · **cannot be used to enter enemy territory**.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Jade Emperor (Yuhuang Dadi) — Mandate of Heaven

Celestial order, supreme authority, divine administration, judgment and hierarchy.

**City passive:** Order and administrative efficiency +15% · administrative buildings affect a larger area · **competent rulers increase the bonuses; incompetent ones reduce part of them**.
*Style: centralized administration.*

**Attack — Decree of Celestial Extermination** (up to 5×5, square). Marks all enemies in the area · reduces attack, defense and speed · removes leadership bonuses · prevents receiving new temporary buffs · allied attacks deal additional damage against the transgressors · the most important enemy commander takes an amplified penalty.
*Escalation of the decree:* **the longer the enemy remains in the area, the greater the attribute reduction, the greater the damage taken and the lower their morale**.

**Defense — Mandate of Heaven** (up to 5×5, square). Increases defense and morale · grants resistance to fear and confusion · amplifies commander bonuses · prevents the immediate elimination of leaders · units near commanders gain additional protection.
*Celestial succession:* if a commander is defeated, the highest-ranking ally assumes their role, part of the leadership bonuses transfers and **the formation suffers no immediate morale penalty**.

**Mobility — Gates of the Celestial Palace** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports all valid allied units · ignores terrain, walls and elevation · **can connect two distant allied districts** · they keep formation · they arrive with Order, vision and resistance bonuses · commanders arrive with part of their abilities recharged.
*Limitations:* the destination must be visible or under allied control · does not transport structures · enemy celestial barriers may prevent the opening.

## Dragon Kings (Longwang) — Rivers of the Four Seas

Seas, rivers, rains, storms and dominion over the waters in different directions.

**City passive:** Water, irrigation, fishing and port production +15% · reservoirs store 20% more water · droughts have reduced duration.
*Style: water, agriculture and navigation.*

**Attack — Deluge of the Four Seas** (up to 5×5, square/line/cone). High damage · pushes enemies back · knocks down light units · damages siege engines · extinguishes fires · reduces the speed of land units · temporarily turns the terrain into a flooded area.
*Epicenter:* amplified damage · **may drag units** · temporarily disables heavy machines.

**Defense — Scales of the Dragon Kings** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Reduces physical damage · strongly reduces fire damage · removes burns · prevents charges · slowly restores buildings · aquatic or amphibious units gain additional regeneration · **projectiles lose force crossing the area**.
*Environmental effect:* if the area is over water or flooded terrain, the protection is amplified.

**Mobility — Rivers in the Sky** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports troops and vessels · ignores walls and elevated terrain · **allows movement over land areas** · aquatic units gain additional speed · the route cannot be blocked by enemy units · the landing creates a small temporary flood.
*Additional effect:* allies arrive resistant to fire and knockback, with vigor partly restored.

## Yanluo Wang (King Yan) — Judgment of the Dead

Judgment of the dead, the register of souls, punishment and administration of the underworld.

**City passive:** Order +15% · crime and corruption are detected faster · cemeteries and tribunals produce Faith · **unjust convictions cause greater Satisfaction penalties**.
*Style: justice, death and discipline.*

**Attack — Final Sentence** (up to 5×5, square). **Enemies with very low health are executed** · the rest take spiritual damage · applies fear · reduces healing and morale · criminal, cursed units or those who have killed many allies take additional damage.
*Judgment by condition:* **Guilty** — high damage · **Condemned** — execution if below the health threshold · **Pending** — attribute reduction and spiritual mark.

**Defense — Register of the Living** (up to 5×5, square). Allies cannot die immediately · **fatal damage is converted into pending damage** · healing received during the ability reduces the pending damage · resistance to fear · deaths cause less morale loss · spiritual allies gain additional protection.
*End of effect:* part of the pending damage is applied — sufficiently healed units survive, those that received no healing may die.

**Mobility — Road of Souls** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Units become **intangible** while moving · they pass through units, walls and buildings · they cannot be attacked by physical weapons during part of the route · they ignore difficult terrain · they arrive camouflaged in spiritual mist · enemies near the destination suffer fear.
*Limitations:* they cannot attack while intangible · **spiritual effects can still reach them during the route** · siege engines cannot be transported.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| Deity | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Shennong** — Bitter Herbs | Farms and medicinal herbs −10% · **agricultural workers fall ill frequently** · research of new crops 20% slower |
| **Chang'e and Houyi** — Misaligned Sky | Hunters −10% during the day · Faith and Satisfaction −10% at night · watchposts lose range |
| **Xihe/Taiyang Shen** — Clouded Sun | Daytime work speed −10% · farms and solar buildings produce no extra · **cold penalties double** |
| **Chiyou** — Blind Weapons | Weapon production and Military strength −20% · newly recruited troops start with no experience · **the elevated army upkeep remains** |
| **Jiutian Xuannü** — Strategy Lost | Military research and defensive efficiency −20% · no information about enemy armies · preparing troops before battle grants no bonus |
| **Zao Jun** — Cold Hearth | Residential Satisfaction −20% · houses consume 20% more fuel · districts with sufficient food stop producing Faith |
| **Jade Emperor** — Mandate Withdrawn | Order and administrative efficiency −30% · administrative buildings affect a reduced area · **competent rulers stop amplifying bonuses; incompetent ones worsen the penalty** |
| **Dragon Kings** — Sealed Seas | Water, irrigation, fishing and ports −30% · reservoirs store 40% less water · **droughts last twice as long** |
| **Yanluo Wang** — Torn Register | Order −30% · **crime and corruption stop being detected** · cemeteries and tribunals generate no Faith · convictions cause amplified Satisfaction loss |

## Strategic Identity

**Shennong** poisons, healing and resistance to negative effects · **Chang'e and Houyi** precision, lunar protection and individual aerial mobility · **Xihe and Taiyang Shen** solar damage, vitality and straight-line movement · **Chiyou** direct confrontation, physical resilience and breaking formations · **Jiutian Xuannü** planning, coordination and tactical repositioning · **Zao Jun** defense of urban areas, protection of buildings and safe retreat · **Jade Emperor** authority, leadership, collective weakening and strategic movement · **Dragon Kings** terrain control, aquatic combat and mass transport · **Yanluo Wang** execution, control of death and spiritual infiltration.
