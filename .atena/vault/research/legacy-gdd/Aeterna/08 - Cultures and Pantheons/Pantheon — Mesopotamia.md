# Copper Age — Single Target

## Dumuzi — Shepherd of the Seasons

Herding, fertility, vegetation, seasonal cycles, death and the return of life.

**City passive:** Herd and food production +5% · animals breed 10% faster · **periods of drought temporarily reduce the bonus**.
*Style: herding and fertility.*

**Attack — Horn of the Divine Shepherd.** Physical damage · pushes the target one cell if the space is free · temporarily reduces its defense · mounted or light units may be knocked down · additional damage if the target is in open field.

**Defense — Return of Spring.** Restores health and vigor · removes exhaustion · temporary regeneration · regeneration amplified near fields, pastures or herds.
*Special effect:* if the protected unit takes fatal damage during the regeneration, it remains at minimum health and the effect ends.

**Mobility — Migration of the Shepherd.** Greatly increased speed · reduces vigor consumption · ignores field, pasture and open terrain penalties · passes through herds and allied units · resistance to slow during movement.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · does not transport siege engines · **lower efficiency in dense urban terrain**.

## Ninurta — Warrior Plowman

Agriculture, war, hunting, storms and combat against monsters and chaotic forces.

**City passive:** Agricultural production and efficiency against hostile creatures +5% · farming tools have greater durability · farmers can assist in defense without heavy penalty.
*Style: defensive agriculture.*

**Attack — Mace of Sharur.** High damage · ignores part of armor · additional damage against monsters, hostile animals and giant units · **interrupts abilities being cast** · additional structural damage against siege engines.

**Defense — Guardian of the Fields.** Increases defense · resistance against creatures and mounted units · reduces damage taken while the target is near a farm, canal or granary · slowly restores tool and equipment durability · prevents knockback from animals and monsters.

**Mobility — Path of the Plow.** Moves the target quickly in a straight line · removes low vegetation and fragile obstacles · ignores mud and farmland · crosses light barricades · the first attack after the movement deals additional damage.
*Limitations:* does not cross walls · the path needs a clear direction · **does not turn**.

## Utu / Shamash — Light of Justice

The Sun, justice, truth, judgment and revelation.

**City passive:** Order +5% · tribunals and administrators resolve conflicts 10% faster · corruption is revealed more often.
*Style: justice and administration.*

**Attack — Ray of Judgment.** Luminous damage · removes camouflage, invisibility and illusions · reduces defense · reveals the target's crimes, curses and hidden effects · **enemies who have broken oaths or attacked civilians take additional damage**.
*Daylight effect:* by day the damage increases, the range increases and the revelation lasts longer.

**Defense — Scales of Truth.** Removes fear, confusion and manipulation · increases resistance · prevents penalties from accusations, intrigue or demoralization · the first unjustified attack against the target deals reduced damage · the aggressor is revealed and marked.
*Thematic condition:* if the ally is under penalty for having broken a law or oath, the ability removes only external effects and **does not lift the legitimate punishment**.

**Mobility — Path of the Sun.** Greatly increased speed · reveals traps and hidden enemies along the route · removes darkness, fear and disorientation · prevents ambushes during movement · additional vision on arrival.
*Limitations:* does not cross major obstacles · the destination must be in a revealed area · **at night the range is reduced**.

# Bronze Age — AoE up to 3×3

## Nergal / Erra — Scourge of War

War, destruction, plagues, extreme heat, violent death and devastation.

**City passive:** Military strength +10% · defeated enemies recover more slowly · **diseases within the city itself become slightly more dangerous**.
*Style: devastating war and risk.*

**Attack — Scourge of Erra** (up to 3×3, square). Physical and spiritual damage · applies disease · reduces healing received · lowers morale · wounded units take additional damage · **corpses in the area extend the disease's duration**.
*Persistent effect:* the struck cells remain contaminated for a short period — enemies entering suffer reduced recovery and healers work with lower efficiency.

**Defense — March of the Condemned** (up to 3×3, square). High resistance · **units with low health deal more damage** · reduces the impact of fear and disease · fatally struck units keep fighting for a short period · deaths within the area temporarily increase the survivors' strength.
*Consequence:* on ending, units saved from death take part of the avoided damage and the area suffers a small temporary Health penalty.

**Mobility — Advance of the Plague** (origin up to 3×3, path of up to 3 cells). Moves all valid allied units quickly · enemies passed through receive disease · allies are partially hidden by the mist · reduces damage taken during the advance · leaves a temporarily contaminated path.
*Limitations:* does not cross complete walls · **allies without disease resistance lose a small amount of health** · does not transport heavy machines.

## Inanna / Ishtar — Power, Love and Conquest

Love, fertility, desire, political power, war, kingship and conquest.

**City passive:** Prestige and population growth +10% · military victories and festivals grant Satisfaction · diplomatic relations begin with greater interest in the city.
*Style: expansion, diplomacy and passion.*

**Attack — Ecstasy of Conquest** (up to 3×3, square). Moderate spiritual damage · lowers morale · applies fascination · fascinated enemies may interrupt attacks · some units may attack nearby allies · commanders take an additional leadership penalty.
*Epicenter:* the central unit becomes unable to give orders and takes increased damage from units led by women, priests or champions.

**Defense — Mantle of the Queen of Heaven** (up to 3×3, square). Raises morale and defense · resistance to fear and fascination · elite units gain regeneration · a commander's presence amplifies the bonuses · enemies have lower accuracy attacking the area.
*Political effect:* units of loyal clans receive additional protection.

**Mobility — Descent and Return** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Transports units between two positions · ignores units and low obstacles · removes immobilization and entrapment · **the units vanish temporarily before returning** · on arrival they gain attack and morale bonuses.
*Cost:* each unit temporarily loses part of its defense, representing the attributes abandoned during the descent.

## Gibil / Nusku — Civilizing Flame

Gibil: fire, the forge, purification and metallurgy. Nusku: household fire, night light, protection and the delivery of divine messages.

**City passive:** Smelters, forges and fuel production +10% · controlled fires and rituals produce Faith · accidental fires are fought 15% faster.
*Style: fire, industry and night protection.*

**Attack — Purifying Furnace** (up to 3×3, square/cross/line). Fire damage · applies burning · reduces the armor of metallic units · damages siege engines and buildings · **removes corruption, shadow or infestation effects from the area** · the epicenter stays aflame longer.

**Defense — Vigilant Flame of Nusku** (up to 3×3, square). Reduces fire and spiritual damage · reveals hidden enemies · prevents ambushes · removes fear caused by darkness · extinguishes harmful fires in allied buildings · enemies entering are revealed.
*Night effect:* at night the area grants greater vision, defense increases and the duration is extended.

**Mobility — Torches of the Messenger** (origin and destination up to 3×3). Greatly increased speed · illuminates the whole route · reveals traps · prevents fear and disorientation · units keep their formation · on arrival they gain temporary fire resistance.
*Limitations:* does not cross great walls · the route must be continuous · **nearby dry forests may catch fire if unprotected**.

# Iron Age — AoE up to 5×5

## Marduk — Sovereignty of the City

Sovereignty, order, creation, authority over the gods and victory against chaotic forces.

**City passive:** Order, Prestige and administrative efficiency +15% · the capital gains a bonus for each subordinate settlement · external rebellions take longer to start.
*Style: empire and centralization.*

**Attack — Net of Tiamat** (up to 5×5, square). Winds push enemies toward the center · reduces speed · **prevents flight and teleportation** · removes formation bonuses · the epicenter takes high damage · chaotic, monstrous or summoned units take additional damage · at the end, a burst of wind knocks down the survivors.
*Territorial control:* during the effect, enemy projectiles have reduced accuracy, flying units are pulled to the ground and enemy summons lose duration.

**Defense — Wall of Celestial Babylon** (up to 5×5, square or ring). Strongly increases the defense of allies and buildings · resistance to fear, confusion and knockback · **buildings cannot lose more than a limited percentage of integrity per attack** · commanders amplify their bonuses · enemies inside the area lose Prestige and leadership efficiency · chaotic summons take continuous damage.

**Mobility — Roads of the King of Gods** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports a large formation between controlled areas keeping relative positions · **can link city, fort, temple or subordinate settlement** · units cannot be intercepted · on arrival they gain Order, morale and defense bonuses · troops from subordinate cities arrive without travel penalty.
*Limitations:* origin and destination must be under allied control · cannot enter an enemy fortification directly · structures are not transported.

## Enki / Ea — Waters of Wisdom

Fresh water, wisdom, magic, creation, the arts, intelligence and the protection of humanity.

**City passive:** Research, irrigation and water production +15% · canals cost 15% fewer resources · part of the losses caused by drought or production failure is recovered.
*Style: knowledge, water and engineering.*

**Attack — Overflowing Abzu** (up to 5×5, square/line/cone). Moderate damage · pushes enemies back · reduces speed · extinguishes fires · **dispels curses, poisons and corrupted areas** · siege engines lose efficiency · the terrain stays flooded temporarily.
*Special effect:* magical or summoned enemies lose part of their bonuses, have their ability durations reduced and may be silenced at the epicenter.

**Defense — Secret of Life** (up to 5×5, square). Gradual healing · removes poison, disease and curse · reduces spiritual and magical damage · vigor regeneration · the first negative effect applied to each unit is nullified · hydraulic buildings in the area restore integrity.
*Adaptive effect:* the protection grows stronger against the damage type most received during the activation.

**Mobility — Canals of the Abzu** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports troops on freshwater currents · ignores difficult terrain, moats and elevation · **crosses small walls through underground passages** · units arrive with vigor restored · extinguishes burns · the path remains a temporary canal usable by reinforcements.
*Limitations:* does not cross deep magical barriers · very heavy machines move slower in the canal · the canal vanishes after the duration.

## Ereshkigal — Queen of the Great Earth

The underworld, the dead, subterranean power and authority over the land of no return.

**City passive:** Mining and funerary stability +15% · deaths cause less Satisfaction loss · necropolises raise the Order and Faith of nearby districts · underground workers suffer fewer accidents.
*Style: underground, death and stability.*

**Attack — Decree of the Great Earth** (up to 5×5, square). Spiritual damage · reduces speed · reduces healing received · applies fear · **defeated units are trapped as shades in place** · the shades reduce the attributes of nearby enemies · the epicenter may temporarily imprison badly wounded units.
*Cursed terrain:* the area stays bound to the underworld — enemies lose morale, enemy resurrections are prevented and allied spiritual units gain bonuses.

**Defense — Throne of the Underworld** (up to 5×5, square). High resistance to spiritual damage · deaths cause less morale loss · **fatally struck units become protective shades** that block attacks and pursue invaders · living allies recover health when an enemy shade is destroyed · enemies cannot summon or control the dead within the area.

**Mobility — Road of No Return** (origin and destination up to 5×5). Transports allies through the underworld · ignores walls, buildings, rivers and terrain · they cannot be intercepted · enemies near the destination suffer fear · allies arrive as shades with temporary camouflage · spiritual units gain a speed bonus on arrival.
*Thematic cost:* living units lose a small amount of morale; **the penalty is removed if they return by the same path before the ability ends**; undead and spiritual units are exempt.

## Wrath of the Abandoned God

Applied for **24 hours** when the city switches to a different culture of the same Descent. It is the inverse of the passive, at double the magnitude. See [[Deities and Passives]].

| God | Wrath |
|---|---|
| **Dumuzi** — Lost Season | Herds and food −10% · animals breed 20% slower · **any drought worsens the penalty rather than merely reducing a bonus** |
| **Ninurta** — Open Fields | Agricultural production and efficiency against hostile creatures −10% · farming tools break faster · farmers cannot assist in defense |
| **Utu/Shamash** — Blind Justice | Order −10% · tribunals resolve conflicts 20% slower · **corruption stops being revealed** |
| **Nergal/Erra** — Scourge Turned | Military strength −20% · defeated enemies recover faster · **diseases within the city itself become far more dangerous** |
| **Inanna/Ishtar** — Desire Withdrawn | Prestige and population growth −20% · victories and festivals grant no Satisfaction · diplomatic relations begin hostile |
| **Gibil/Nusku** — Flame Extinguished | Smelters, forges and fuel production −20% · fire rituals generate no Faith · accidental fires take twice as long to fight |
| **Marduk** — Order Undone | Order, Prestige and administrative efficiency −30% · **the capital loses the bonuses of every subordinate settlement** · external rebellions begin at once |
| **Enki/Ea** — Waters Withdrawn | Research, irrigation and water production −30% · canals cost 30% more resources · losses from drought or production failure are not recovered |
| **Ereshkigal** — Sealed Earth | Mining −30% · deaths cause double Satisfaction loss · **necropolises reduce the Order and Faith of nearby districts** · underground workers suffer frequent accidents |

## Strategic Identity

**Dumuzi** sustain, regeneration and mobility in open terrain · **Ninurta** combat against monsters, agricultural protection and frontal advance · **Utu/Shamash** revelation, justice, removal of deception and safe movement · **Nergal/Erra** gradual damage, disease, attrition and aggressive advance · **Inanna/Ishtar** emotional manipulation, leadership and repositioning with risk · **Gibil/Nusku** fire, purification, vigilance and night movement · **Marduk** centralization, control of large areas and combat against chaotic forces · **Enki/Ea** healing, water, defensive magic and route creation · **Ereshkigal** fear, death, spiritual units and infiltration through the underworld.
