# Bandits and Full Loot
**Category:** [[05 - Combat and Factions]]

## The Underworld (Unofficial Settlements)
Not every player wants to live under the Council's laws. It is possible to build clandestine camps (Bands) in the open world to store resources looted from [[Caravan Logistics]].
* **Hideout:** These camps do not appear on the global map and depend on geographic isolation (fog of war) to survive.

## The Risk of Criminality
* **Raids:** Unlike elite zones, bandit camps have no protection. If discovered, they can be completely destroyed and looted by citizens seeking to recover their goods.
* **Bounty System:** Players can put their coins up as collateral to issue hunting contracts against thieves and mercenaries from other cities, encouraging targeted PvP.
