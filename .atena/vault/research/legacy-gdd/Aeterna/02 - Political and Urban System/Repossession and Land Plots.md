# Repossession and Land Plots
**Category:** [[02 - Political and Urban System]]

## The Mansion Graveyard (Anti-Stagnation)
To prevent millionaire players from abandoning the game and locking up Elite Zone land, property requires constant upkeep.

## Eviction Triggers
The player loses the house and the plot returns to Public Auction if they:
1. Fail to pay the weekly tax.
2. Commit serious crimes against the city, zeroing their Reputation.
3. Go inactive (Decay) beyond the stipulated day limit.

## The Eviction Vault
When eviction happens, the items inside the house are not deleted immediately. They go to a temporary vault (Limbo). The player has a deadline to pay a very high fine and recover the assets, or the items are permanently destroyed by the server, easing inflation.
