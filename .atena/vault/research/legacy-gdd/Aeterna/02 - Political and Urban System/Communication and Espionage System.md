# Communication and Espionage System
**Category:** [[02 - Political and Urban System]] (with impact on [[Caravan Logistics]])

## Local Communication
Access to communication depends strictly on geography and on the level of civilization of the area.
* **Open World (Proximity Voice):** Outside city borders there is no text chat. The only form of communication is directional proximity VoIP. Speaking loudly reveals your position to enemies, bandits or scouts hidden in the fog of war.
* **Urban Zones (The Refuge of Text):** Upon crossing the borders of an official city, the player connects to the local network and the city's Text Chat is unlocked, enabling trade, political debate and public announcements on the Town Hall Board.

## Physical Diplomacy (Letters and Emissaries)
Official communication between two different cities cannot happen instantly. Information travels at the speed of a player.
* **The "Letter" Item:** Diplomatic messages, declarations or agreements become physical items (Letters) in an Emissary's inventory.
* **Interception Risk:** Like any other cargo, the Emissary can be ambushed en route. If killed, the letter can be intercepted, stolen and never reach its destination, causing communication failures or leaking state secrets to enemy factions.

## Oral Cultures and the Gathering Point

In the Stone Age, before any writing exists, all decisions are made by **in-person voting** at the Gathering Point — see [[Era Progression]].

Cultures that never develop writing (Yoruba, Tupi-Guarani) never leave that constraint behind. They gain immunity to record destruction and have no Letters to intercept, but they cannot govern at a distance. See [[The Town Hall and the Council]].
