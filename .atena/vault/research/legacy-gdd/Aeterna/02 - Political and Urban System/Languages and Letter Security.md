# Languages and Letter Security
**Category:** [[02 - Political and Urban System]] (with impact on [[Caravan Logistics]])

## The Language Barrier (Cultural Encryption)
The world of Aetherna has no unified global language. Language is an instrument of the State.
* **Literacy Through Citizenship:** Upon completing the Trial Stage and becoming a Citizen of a city, the player automatically learns the local language.
* **Illegibility to Outsiders:** If a bandit, nomad or foreign citizen steals or intercepts a Letter, they are physically incapable of reading its contents in the interface, seeing only incomprehensible characters.

## The Value of Intercepted Information
Even without being able to read it, the interceptor holds the physical item (the Letter). They have full systemic freedom to:
1. **Destroy the item:** erasing the information from the server and sabotaging the plans of the cities involved.
2. **Smuggle it:** selling the "sealed" letter on the black market to rival cities that may have the means to translate it (through infiltrated spies).

## Military Intelligence Logistics
Given the high risk of interception and the catastrophic impact of a leak (such as revealing the plans of [[War and Expansion Policy]]), transporting important Letters cannot be done by a lone emissary. High-level documents demand the same logistics, route planning and armed escort as a caravan carrying gold.

## Descent, Culture and Language

The language a city speaks follows its **culture**, not its Descent — see [[Culture System]]. This has a consequence that has not yet been decided: when a city switches culture at an era transition, does its language change with it, invalidating every Letter written under the previous culture?

Cultures **without writing** — Yoruba and Tupi-Guarani — sit outside this system entirely. They produce no Letters, so they have nothing to intercept. See the writing advantage section in [[The Town Hall and the Council]].
