# Urban Zoning and Elite Zones
**Category:** [[02 - Political and Urban System]]

## The Housing Hierarchy
The city's architecture reflects the social pyramid. When [[The Town Hall and the Council]] founds the city, the urban grid around Ground Zero is divided, respecting streets, elevation changes, walls and strategic access points.

* **Common Districts:** Peripheral, cheaper plots. Subject to expropriation if the Council decides to reshape the city.
* **Elite Zones (Immovable):** Premium land near the center. Extremely high-cost properties, immune to Council expropriation. They grant the largest stamina recovery and prestige bonuses.

## Scarcity Economy
Elite Zones exist in strictly limited quantity on the map. The only way to acquire one is through a Town Hall Auction (which drains a great deal of gold from the server) or when the previous owner loses the property under the rules of [[Repossession and Land Plots]].
