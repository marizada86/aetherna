# War and Expansion Policy
**Category:** [[02 - Political and Urban System]]

## Casus Belli (War Triggers)
The server does not allow wars without mathematical justification. For the Council to issue a War Petition, historical attrition goals must be met.
Valid triggers:
* 100 recorded murders between citizens in the border region.
* Formal breach of 10 trade treaties.
* Destruction of 50 of the city's trade wagons.

## Petition Types and Espionage
For war to be declared, a Petition must be drafted by the Councilors, validated among them and submitted to a popular vote (only for Citizens physically inside the Town Hall).
* **Open War (Surrender and Merger):** An emissary carries the terms of surrender to the enemy city. If accepted, war is avoided. The defeated city is absorbed and its residents enter a **Trial Stage** (working without political rights until they reclaim Citizenship). Offices and the previous culture cease to exist.

    Because the previous culture ceases to exist, the absorbed population lives under the **deity of the victorious city** — and, with it, under a different structure of government. See [[The Town Hall and the Council]].
* **Secret War:** No emissary is sent, only the surprise attack.
* **The Reality of Espionage:** The risk of a Secret War leaking during the vote is real. Enemy spies can infiltrate the city (by obtaining false citizenship) to access the Town Hall and read the votes. Information is a weapon.

## Effect on Ultimates

The divine ult belongs to the deity, and the deity belongs to the city — see [[3-Ability Combat System]].

* A **citizen in the Trial Stage** keeps their 3 profession abilities (permanent, in the [[Grimoire]]) but **loses the ult of the defeated city**, and only receives the victorious city's ult once they reclaim Citizenship.
* Absorbing a city of **another Descent** means its residents can never return to their former identity: the absorbed city's Descent ceases to exist along with the city.
* A city that loses a war and is absorbed **does not suffer the Wrath of the Abandoned God** — wrath punishes voluntary blasphemy, not conquest.
