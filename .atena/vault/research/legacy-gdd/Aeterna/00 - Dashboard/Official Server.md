# Official Server
**Category:** [[00 - Dashboard]]
**Status:** Out of scope

## Decision

The project does **not** operate, host or sustain a persistent official server. This is explicitly out of scope.

Aetherna is Open Source: the authoritative server is part of the distributed code and is stood up by the community itself.

## Consequences in the GDD

* **Economy:** there is no revenue share on the internal economy. See [[The Tax and Gold System]].
* **Seasons and wipes:** they stop being a central calendar and become per-server and per-Lore configuration. See [[Seasons and Hard Wipes]].
* **Relics and account progression:** persistence across seasons is the scope of each server, not guaranteed globally.
* **Monetization:** the project's funding does not depend on running a server. See [[Open Source Model and Monetization]].

## Open Questions

* Is there a global account identity (for unlocking [[Editor Mode]] and purchases in the [[Lore Market]]) even without an official game server? Probably yes, and it is a service distinct from the match server.
* Discovery of community servers: a public list maintained by the project, or fully decentralized?
