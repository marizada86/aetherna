# Open Source Model and Monetization
**Category:** [[03 - Economy and Market]]

> This note replaces the former `Monetization and VIP House`. The VIP subscription model and the 10% cut on the internal market have been discontinued.

## Core Directive

Aetherna is an **Open Source game**. The playable client is distributed free of charge, and with it a player can already play any Lore created by the community without paying anything.

The project's monetization is deliberately narrow. There are exactly two sources of revenue:

1. Unlocking [[Editor Mode]] — a one-time payment of **R$ 10.00**.
2. A **10%** commission on sales made in the [[Lore Market]].

**There is no other monetization of the game itself.** No subscription, no VIP, no paid cosmetics, no battle pass, no premium currency, no fee on the internal economy of matches.

## The Free Client

The playable executable is free and includes:

* Full access to the game's core loop.
* The ability to run and join community servers.
* The ability to install and play community Lores, free or paid.
* The ability to install mods (see [[Mods and Extensibility]]).

The code is open. Forks, self-hosted servers and alternative distributions are permitted under the project's license.

## Editor Mode (R$ 10.00)

Creating content for the community requires unlocking **Editor Mode** for a one-time payment of R$ 10.00 per account.

Editor Mode is the Lore authoring tool: world, maps, rules, quests, narrative, assets and balancing. Details in [[Editor Mode]].

Playing content created by others never requires Editor Mode. The charge falls on authorship, not on consumption.

## Lore Market

The game launches alongside an **official Lore**. Official Lores are acquired in the [[Lore Market]].

A creator using Editor Mode may publish and sell their Lore in the Market. In that case the game retains **10% of the sale value** and passes 90% to the creator.

Community Lores may also circulate outside the Market, free of charge or under the creator's own rules. In that case the game charges nothing, because it does not broker the transaction.

## Zero Pay-to-Win Rule

The rule still holds and is easier to sustain: since no items, power, time or competitive advantage are sold, there is no pay-to-win vector in the base game. What is sold is an authoring tool and authored content — never an advantage inside a match.

If a community Lore decides to sell advantage inside its own content, that is the decision and responsibility of that Lore's creator, not of the base game.

## Official Server

**Out of scope.** The project does not operate, host or sustain a persistent official server. Servers are stood up by the community from the open source code.

Direct consequence: there is no "game profit sustaining the server". The former 10% slice of market taxes described in [[The Tax and Gold System]] has been removed — the entire collection goes to the city's public treasury.

## VIP House / Mobile Base

The **Mobile Operations Base** is no longer a subscription benefit. It remains a game mechanic, unlockable within the match (crafting/progression), keeping its original balance: 24-hour placement cooldown, 100% vulnerable, destructible by enemies, subject to collision rules and minimum distance from enemy town halls.

The experience bonus that existed in the VIP package has been **removed**. Paid progression acceleration is not compatible with the Open Source model.
