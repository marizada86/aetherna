# The Tax and Gold System
**Category:** [[03 - Economy and Market]]

## Wealth Generation and the Tax
Every transaction in Aetherna's market is subject to a tax. The city's Town Hall collects a percentage of local trade. The revenue goes **entirely** to the public treasury (managed by [[The Town Hall and the Council]]).

The game takes no share of this tax. The former 10% slice ("real game profit, sustaining the server") was removed along with the [[Official Server]], which is out of scope. The project's monetization is described in [[Open Source Model and Monetization]].

## The Auction and Reputation (Gating)
The auction of high-level items and immovable land in the [[Urban Zoning and Elite Zones]] acts as the server's largest currency sink, controlling inflation.
* **Citizenship Filter:** The bidding interface is only unlocked for players with high Reputation (earned by paying taxes, defending the city and working). A wealthy player cannot simply inject money and buy elite property on Day 1.

## The Temple as a Sink

From the Copper Age onward, the temple becomes the city's main systemic sink. The **regular offerings** demanded by several Iron Age deities are the concrete mechanism of that drain — see [[Deities and Passives]].
