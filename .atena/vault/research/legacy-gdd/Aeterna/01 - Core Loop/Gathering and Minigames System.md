# Gathering and Minigames System
**Category:** [[01 - Core Loop]]

## Effort Tiers
The game splits gathering and production (Crafting) into two layers of interaction:

* **Basic Gathering (Tier 0 and 1):** Chopping wood or mining stone requires only a click and stamina expenditure. Speed improves with tool quality.
* **Advanced Forging (Skill-Based):** Producing lethal weapons or complex parts triggers real skill minigames. Item quality depends not only on materials, but on the player's reflexes and precision during the minigame.

## Masterpiece Quality
A perfect hit in the minigame produces an item of exceptional quality (Rare/Epic). That item receives the smith's or artisan's "signature", creating organic monopolies of players famous for their technical skill rather than for accumulated capital. Progress in these trades also unlocks options in the [[3-Ability Combat System]].
