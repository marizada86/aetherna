# The Nomad Phase (Basic Survival)
**Category:** [[01 - Core Loop]]

## The Starting Point
Every player in **Aetherna**, after creating an account or right after a Wipe event from [[Seasons and Hard Wipes]], is born (spawns) in the wilderness as a Nomad.

## The Stamina Mechanic
* **The Engine of Action:** All movement, gathering or combat consumes a Stamina bar (tick rate on the backend via WebSockets).
* **Recovery:** Stamina is only recovered by consuming food or resting in safe properties (see [[Urban Zoning and Elite Zones]] or the Mobile Operations Base in [[Open Source Model and Monetization]]).

## Nomad Punishment (Initial Full Loot)
If the stamina bar hits zero in the open world before the player reaches a city, the character dies of exhaustion.
* **Death:** The server wipes 100% of the player's inventory.
* **Respawn:** The player reappears at random coordinates on the map, restarting the gathering cycle from scratch.

## The Transition to Citizenship
To stop being a Nomad, the player must wander the map until they find the zone of influence of a city (run by players) and interact with [[The Town Hall and the Council]] to buy their first basic land plot.
