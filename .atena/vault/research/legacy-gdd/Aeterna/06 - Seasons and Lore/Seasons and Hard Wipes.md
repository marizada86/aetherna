# Seasons and Hard Wipes
**Category:** [[06 - Seasons and Lore]]

## The Impermanence of Aetherna
This game runs on a **Hardcore Sandbox** model. Infinite accumulation is impossible. The game's value lies in the experience of the climb and in the temporary control of power.

Without an [[Official Server]] in scope, seasons and wipes stop being a central calendar and become configuration of each community server and each Lore (see [[Lore Market]]). What follows describes the default behavior inherited by the official Lore.

## The Narrative Engine
Seasons do not end on a fixed mathematical timer, but according to the progression of the narrative generated by the players themselves in the world.
* **Cataclysm:** When the season reaches its peak, a live event (meteors, floods, magical corruption) destroys all structures.
* **The Great Reset:** Cities, bank balances, vaults and inventories are erased. All players return to the status of [[The Nomad Phase]].

## Relics (Permanent Progression)
The only things that survive the Wipe are Titles and Cosmetic Relics granted to the most prominent leaders, warriors and mayors of the previous era. They act as permanent prestige medals tied to the account.

## What the Wipe Does to Identity

The wipe erases the city, and with it the city's **Descent**, culture and patron deity. Since the divine ult belongs to the city and not the player (see [[3-Ability Combat System]]), it does not survive the wipe either.

The [[Grimoire]] preserves the 3 profession abilities. The fourth slot starts empty every season, until the player joins a new city that reaches the Copper Age and chooses a deity.
