# Lore Market
**Category:** [[06 - Seasons and Lore]]

## What a Lore Is

A Lore is a complete playable content package: world, rules, narrative, economy and assets. The free client runs any Lore.

## Launch Lore

The game **launches alongside an official Lore**. It does not ship embedded in the free client: it is acquired in the Lore Market, like any other.

## Official Lores

Produced by the project. Sold exclusively in the Lore Market.

## Community Lores

Anyone with [[Editor Mode]] can create and distribute Lores. Two routes:

**1. Publish in the Lore Market.** The game brokers the sale and retains **10% of the value**. The creator receives 90%. The creator sets the price, including free.

**2. Distribute outside the Market.** Permitted. Free or under the creator's own rules. The game charges nothing and does not broker.

## Fee Summary

| Origin | Game commission |
|---|---|
| Official Lore | Full project revenue |
| Community Lore sold in the Market | 10% |
| Community Lore distributed outside the Market | 0% |
| Free community Lore | 0% |

See [[Open Source Model and Monetization]].
