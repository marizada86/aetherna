# Caravan Logistics
**Category:** [[04 - Logistics and Open World]]

## End of the Magic Inventory
There is no Fast Travel in Aetherna. Every resource must be physically transported. Players do not carry tons of stone, ore or wood in their pockets; moving cargo requires physically crafting Wagons and Caravans.

## Transport Dynamics
* **Physical Weight:** The total weight of the cargo affects the vehicle's movement speed.
* **Fixed Routes and Tactical Chokepoints:** Caravans navigate pre-drawn routes, producing predictable and contestable chokepoints. Biome transition zones become natural ambush points — emergent from geography, not hand-placed. See [[Visual and Mechanical Direction]] for how terrain verticality shapes these routes.

## Full Loot in the Open World
If the caravan is intercepted and destroyed, **100% of the cargo is exposed in the wreckage** for looting by any player.
* **Systemic Countermeasure:** Merchants must hire **mercenaries** (players with advanced combat abilities from the [[3-Ability Combat System]]) and **scouts**. This turns the economy into an employer of PvP — one of the game's central economic loops.
