# Style bible (v1)

Base: DEC-005. Cole o **BLOCO DE ESTILO** inteiro, sem alterar, no início de todo prompt de personagem.

## BLOCO DE ESTILO (inglês, colar sempre igual)

```
Character concept art for a stylized painterly 3D action-RPG, isometric top-down game, dark-fantasy oil-painting look: visible brush strokes, hand-painted textures, muted earthy palette (bronze gold, ochre, madder red, indigo, olive, bone white), soft dramatic lighting with warm rim light, slightly stylized realistic proportions. Setting: Late Bronze Age Mycenaean Greece and Anatolia, around 1200 BC. Single full-body character, standing in a relaxed neutral pose facing slightly to the viewer's left (3/4 view), arms slightly away from the body, centered, on a plain flat neutral light-gray background, no cast shadow on the ground, no scenery, no text, no watermark, no border. Whole figure visible from head to sandals.
```

## BLOCO DE NEGATIVOS (colar sempre no fim)

```
Do NOT include: Corinthian helmet, hoplite round shield with arm-band, muscle cuirass, Roman or medieval armor, Greek classical chlamys/himation, Phrygian cap, Greek key (meander) borders, palmette or anthemion patterns (use Mycenaean running spirals, rosettes and wave bands instead), helmets with a nose guard or any Attic, Chalcidian or Corinthian shape, steel or silver-looking armor (bronze must look gold-brassy), modern objects, text, logos, multiple characters, background scenery, extra weapons not listed.
```

## Regras
- Uma imagem por personagem; aprovada antes de seguir (DEC-004).
- Cada rodada mexe em **uma** coisa (roupa, rosto, pose), citando o que manter.
- Imagem aprovada é anexada como **referência de estilo** nos prompts seguintes: "same painterly style, same lighting and palette as the reference image".
- Salvar como `art/concept/char-<id>-vN.png` e registrar a versão aprovada na ficha.
- Cor de bronze: dourado; prata só nas peças de prata do texto.
