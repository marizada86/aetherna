# Prompt v1: Odisseu (concept)

Ordem de colagem: BLOCO DE ESTILO + BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM + BLOCO DE NEGATIVOS (style-bible.md).


## BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM

```
Odysseus, king of Ithaca, a shorter, stocky Achaean man in his thirties, one head shorter than a tall king but with very broad shoulders, deep barrel chest and comparatively short legs, giving him a squat, powerful build. Weathered sun-tanned skin, thick dark curly beard, thick curly dark-blond hair to the neck. His eyes are lowered toward the ground, thoughtful, shrewd and calm, with the look of a clever man who hides his cunning behind a plain, almost dull expression. He wears a plain but good-quality knee-length short-sleeved wool tunic in ochre with a narrow olive-green band at the hem, a leather belt, and a practical dark olive wool cloak pinned at one shoulder with a small bronze pin. Sturdy laced leather sandals. Hands relaxed, empty. No armor, no helmet, no shield, no weapon. Quiet, watchful, patient presence.
```
