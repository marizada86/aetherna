# Prompt v1: Heitor (concept)

Ordem de colagem: BLOCO DE ESTILO + BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM + BLOCO DE NEGATIVOS (style-bible.md).
Nota: o bloco do personagem sobrepõe "arms slightly away from the body" do estilo, pois ele segura objetos.

## BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM

```
Hector, prince of Troy, a tall, broad-shouldered Trojan warrior in his late twenties, dark hair and a short neat dark beard, handsome, noble face with a serious, watchful expression and intense eyes. He wears a bronze helmet with cheek guards topped with a tall dark horsehair crest, a bronze scale cuirass over a knee-length wool tunic dyed deep teal with cream and gold-trimmed hem, a wide belt, bronze greaves, and a cream wool cloak pinned at one shoulder with a gold pin. A large sheathed bronze sword hangs at his left hip. In his right hand he holds upright a long ash spear with a leaf-shaped bronze head and a gold ring around the socket; a tall body shield made of layered ox-hide with a bronze boss, figure-eight shaped, rests on the ground against his left leg. Trojan palette: teal, cream and gold. Firm, protective, weighed down by duty.
```
