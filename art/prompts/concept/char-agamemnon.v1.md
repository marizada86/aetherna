# Prompt v1: Agamémnon (concept)

Ordem de colagem: BLOCO DE ESTILO + BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM + BLOCO DE NEGATIVOS (style-bible.md).
Nota: o bloco do personagem sobrepõe "arms slightly away from the body" do estilo, pois ele segura objetos.

## BLOCO DO PERSONAGEM

```
Agamemnon, king of kings, a tall, powerfully built mature man in his late forties with a broad chest and commanding, arrogant bearing. Full dark-brown beard with streaks of grey and long dark-brown hair to the shoulders. His face is stern and proud, slightly flushed and puffy with a reddened nose and heavy eyelids, the mark of a heavy wine drinker, yet still noble and authoritative; eyes burning with dark rage. He wears a rich knee-length short-sleeved wool tunic dyed deep purple with gold-thread embroidered bands at the hem and sleeves, a wide leather belt with bronze and gold fittings, a short dagger at the belt, and a heavy dark-blue wool cloak pinned at one shoulder with a large ornate gold pin. Leather sandals with gold-studded straps. In his right hand he holds upright the royal sceptre: a tall staff studded with gold nails, more ornate than any ordinary sceptre, the butt resting on the ground. No armor, no helmet, no shield. Imposing, rigid, intolerant of contradiction.
```
